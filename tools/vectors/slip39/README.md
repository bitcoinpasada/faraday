# SLIP-39 test vectors

`vectors.json` verbatim from Trezor's reference implementation,
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trezor/python-shamir-mnemonic/master/vectors.json`.
To refresh it, download it again and check the digest changed for a
reason.

| File | Downloaded | SHA-256 | Read by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `vectors.json` | 2026-09-18 | `13ebecebdd869dd2bc2cdf69e7ce3a158cf106cac76c39d17682b1c6cdabbdc4` | `core/osk-bip/tests/slip39.rs` |

The file is 45 cases, each an array of four: a description, the share
mnemonics, the master secret in hex — empty when the case must be
refused — and the BIP-32 root key. The passphrase throughout is
`TREZOR`. Fifteen cases recover; thirty are refused, each for a named
reason: a bad checksum, non-zero padding, a share value of the wrong
length, shares from different backups, mismatched thresholds or counts,
a duplicate member index, a digest that does not match, too few groups
or too few members of a group. Cases 42 to 45 are extendable backups,
whose master secret encryption does not salt with the identifier.

The root key is the master secret handed straight to
`bitcoin::bip32::Xpriv::new_master`. It is the vectors' own statement
that a SLIP-39 master secret is the BIP-32 seed and not BIP-39 entropy.

## The wordlist

The 1024 words are not in this directory: they are embedded in
`core/osk-bip/src/wordlists/slip39.rs`, generated by
`tools/wordlists/gen-slip39.py` from
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/satoshilabs/slips/master/slip-0039/wordlist.txt`
(downloaded 2026-09-18, SHA-256
`bcc4555340332d169718aed8bf31dd9d5248cb7da6e5d355140ef4f1e601eec3`).
The digest is recorded in the generated module and
`core/osk-bip/tests/slip39.rs` rebuilds the file's bytes from the
embedded words and re-hashes them, as the BIP-39 lists are checked.

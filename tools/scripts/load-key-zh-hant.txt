# Walks the Load wizard on the Traditional Chinese list, typing on the
# 注音 keyboard: the 大千 layout, the syllable in bopomofo and then one
# of the five tone marks. ˉ ends the second row, so the first tone is a
# key like the other four and nothing is offered until it is pressed
# (PLANNING §16.43). A character is one glyph, so the strip is ten cells
# a row and two rows, and the largest tone group either list has —
# twenty characters — is on the screen at once (PLANNING §16.45). Widget
# ids are the constants in opensigner/opensigner-core/src/ids.rs.

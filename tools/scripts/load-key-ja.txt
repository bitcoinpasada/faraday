# The mnemonic is the one 0x12 repeated sixteen times encodes; its first
# word takes the 小 key (よ becomes ょ) and its second the ゛ key
# (た becomes だ). No eye here: a Japanese word can be seven kana, which
# is one column of the panel on a phone, so the entry screen carries no
# panel there and no eye with it (docs/PLANNING.md §16.41);
# load-key-ko.txt walks the panel and the eye. Widget ids are the
# constants in opensigner/opensigner-core/src/ids.rs.

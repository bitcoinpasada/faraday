# F225 The open checklist card's header at 1024 × 600 is held at eight under the touch-target floor (2026-11-08)

**Kind:** History
**Status:** Withdrawn
**Area:** design

*Withdrawn (2026-11-09): the step flows' card headers and the keyboard's keys reached the floor, so the two lines it held are gone from `tools/touch-targets.counts`.*

**Why.** A seed's Krux code is a checklist item in the backup flow, and
its tour opens the item's card. On the stick at 1024 × 600 the open card's
header is a press target drawn 25 px tall, under the 48 dp floor, as the
headers of the seven checklist cards the tours already open are. The fix is
one change to the step flows' card headers and the keyboard's keys, which
lowers every one of them; it is later work. The owner chose to hold the
new card with the others until then (owner, 2026-11-08).

**Decision.**

1. **The line `stick_1024x600 Backup BStep` is held at 8**, up from 7: the
   Krux code item's open card, drawn like every other checklist card.
2. **The line `stick_1024x600 Backup Osk` is held at 38**, new: the
   on-screen keyboard over the item's passphrase, the same 38 keys held on
   Convert, the Krux code tool and the backup flow.
3. **The lines only fall from here.** The fix to the card headers and the keys lowers
   them, and this decision is withdrawn when both reach 0.

**Against Material.** Material asks for a 48 × 48 dp target on every
control. The open card's header falls short of it at 1024 × 600, as the
seven held already do, and so do the keyboard's keys; no other size and no
other control is held by this.

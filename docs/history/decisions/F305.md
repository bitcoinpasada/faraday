# F305 Scan is the navigation rail's FAB, at the top of the rail and the bottom left of the sidebar (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** History
**Status:** Replaced by F312
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Replaced by F312 (2026-11-21): Scan is the FAB at the foot of both the rail and the sidebar.*

**Why.** Scan sat far from everything at the bottom right of a wide window.
Material's navigation rail gives the FAB a place at its top, above the
destinations; the expanded rail takes an extended FAB.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21; `docs/LAYOUT.md` §3 and §15, `docs/HOME.md` §1).

- On the expanded rail (the desktop's sidebar) Scan is an extended FAB, the
  icon and the word "Scan" in Material's primary FAB style, only as wide as
  they are, pinned at the sidebar's bottom left where Power off was, and it
  never scrolls away with the destinations. This place is a difference from
  Material, whose expanded rail puts the FAB at the top: the owner chose the
  bottom left because Scan stands where Power off was and stays in reach
  without scrolling. On the rail's windows (the collapsed rail) it is the
  FAB, the icon alone, at the rail's top, where no scrolling is needed to
  find it, and the destinations keep Material's spacing below it.
- It is there on every page of these windows, and no page draws a Scan
  button of its own on them: the floating Scan of Home, Check, Wallets,
  Files, Vaults and Tools is gone from them, and they keep no room at their
  foot for it.
- The Pi's menu and the phone-size window keep Scan floating at the bottom
  right, on the pages that have it today.
- A tool's own Scan in its card, where it has nothing to read, stays: it is
  the tool's.

Follows Material 3 Floating action button and Navigation rail.

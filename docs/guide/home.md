# Home

**Kind:** Reference
**Status:** Checked against the app
**Screen:** `Home`
**Key:** home
**Words:** key, wallet

Home is the first screen after the self-test and after every lock.
It leads with the next thing to do, shows each wallet loaded with its
Sign button, and, while nothing is loaded, the tiles that start each task.

## Search

**Key:** home.search

A search bar stands under Home's buttons (every window but the Pi's small
panel). Typing finds a tool, a setting or a glossary word and shows the matches under the bar,
the buttons above staying; a press opens one, a word at its definition in the guide. Escape clears the search.

# F112 One explanation on a working screen: the tooltip on "codex32" in Make a new wallet's Kind card (2026-10-24)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** strings.no-explanation

**Why.** A working screen carries labels, values and actions, never an
explanation; explanations live in Learn and About (CLAUDE.md). Make a
new wallet's Kind card now opens with Single key, backup in shares
(codex32) ticked as the default (`docs/CODEX32.md` §6.9). A person
meeting that default is choosing a backup they may not have heard of,
and the one fact that sets codex32 apart from every other backup is
short enough to say where the choice is made.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-24).

- The word "codex32" in the Kind row's name, "Single key, backup in
  shares (codex32)", carries a tooltip: "codex32 is the only method that
  makes a seed and its shares fully by hand, the checksum included."
- It is a deliberate exception to the working-screen rule, in this one
  place. No other working screen gains an explanation from it; another
  exception is a decision of its own.
- The tooltip is Material 3's (`docs/DESIGN.md` §4.12, Tooltip), the
  rich one, which Material gives a sentence of explanation; the plain
  one is built beside it for icon-only controls, which name a control
  and explain nothing, so they need no exception.
- Material defines no mark for a word in running text: its tooltips
  attach to a control, and its way of offering more about a term is an
  info icon button beside it. So Material's info icon button (outlined
  "info", the row's secondary ink, its own 48 dp target taking the press
  only on the button) stands right after "(codex32)" (owner,
  2026-10-24).
- It shows as Material's tooltips do: the pointer resting on the button
  or the word, the keyboard on the button, or a long press on either,
  after a short delay; and at once when the button is pressed, as a rich
  tooltip may be opened by its icon. It goes when the pointer leaves,
  the keyboard moves on, at Escape, or at the next press.
- Sources: Android's Compose documentation of Material 3 tooltips and
  Material's Icon buttons guidance; m3.material.io's own pages are
  script-drawn and could not be read.

**What it does not change.** The row's second line stays a fact of the
row (§6.9). Learn's Other backups page keeps the full account of
codex32 and how it compares (`docs/CODEX32.md` §15.3).

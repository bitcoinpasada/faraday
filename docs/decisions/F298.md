# F298 One menu page container: Home, Tools, Check, Wallets start and Settings in one feed (2026-11-20)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** review

*Amends `docs/LAYOUT.md` §4 and §17 for the menu pages: Home's tiles
take Tools' minimum in place of `TILE_MIN`; Settings' cards stand in
the menu page's grid, at the same width and edges as the other menu
pages. The owner's call of 2026-10-25 on Settings
(the list on a compact window, the two columns' fixed split) stands.*

**Why.** The pages a person picks a place or a
tool from each draw their own page. Tools and Check share one grid,
Home has its own grid at a smaller tile, Wallets start copies Tools'
width inline, and Settings has its own two-column rule and width, so
their edges, gaps and column counts differ from page to page. One
container should draw them, as the flow container draws every flow
(F296).

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-20; `docs/MENU-PAGE.md`).

1. **Five pages, one container.** Home, Tools, Check, Wallets start and
   Settings are drawn by one menu page container, Material 3's feed
   layout. Vault contents is not one of them: it is list and detail.
2. **What a page gives.** Its name, whether it has the search bar, and
   its sections in order, each a label and its tiles, its cards or a
   block across the page. The container owns the page's width and
   centring, the search bar's place, the column count, the spacing and
   the one-column layouts' path.
3. **One tile minimum.** Every menu page's tile is at least
   `CATALOG_TILE_MIN` (360 dp) wide, Tools' minimum; Home's tiles take
   it. Checked at 480 × 640 and at 1920 × 1080 before it is settled; a
   difference found there goes back to the owner.
4. **Settings' cards** stand in the container's grid, one column on a
   narrow page and two where there is room, cards of different heights
   allowed, at the same width and edges as the other menu pages.

**Recommended in the Spec, for the owner's answer:**

- The column floor goes (`CATALOG_COLUMNS_MIN`, Tools' and Check's at
  least three beside the navigation): columns are what the page holds at
  the minimum, at least one, as Material's adaptive grid has it. The
  floor is what draws Tools' tiles under 360 dp today.
- Settings' columns are no longer capped at `COLUMN_MAX` each: at two
  in the menu page's width they stay under it.
- The page is at most `MENU_PAGE_W`, three tiles at the minimum and two
  gaps (1112 dp), in place of `TRANSFER_W` (1100), which holds only two;
  one gap, `MENU_GAP` (16 dp), across and down, for tiles and cards.
- The page's top at `PANE_TOP_BAR` on every menu page and its foot at
  `MARGIN_WIDE`.
- Tools' search bar stays still while the page scrolls, as Home's does.
- The Pi's panel menu keeps its places two to a row.

**Checked against Material 3.**

- *Feed*, as Android's "Canonical layouts" (developer.android.com,
  large screens) states it (Material's own feed page,
  m3.material.io, could not be read when this was written): "A feed layout arranges
  equivalent content elements in a configurable grid", adapting "from a
  single, scrolling column to a multi-column scrolling feed of content";
  the sample sizes its grid by a minimum item width
  (`GridCells.Adaptive`), not a fixed column count.
- *Search bar*: the check already recorded for Home's
  (`docs/LAYOUT.md` §4, Material's Search bar).

**Where Faraday differs from Material**, each named:

- **A page width.** Material's feed guidance names no maximum
  width; the menu page stops at `MENU_PAGE_W`, so its tiles do not grow without end on a
  large monitor and the five pages share their edges.
- **Settings' fixed split.** Its cards stack in two columns with a
  break the page names, not in Material's flowing grid, so a card never
  moves between columns as the window changes.
- **The Pi's panel menu**, two places to a row narrower than the
  minimum, since the panel is the navigation itself.

# F146 One stick numbering, "Back to" under a page's name, and the codex32 entry's step in the top app bar (2026-10-28)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

**Why.** Three things on screen read two ways. The backup board
numbered each place's sticks from 1 (`docs/BACKUP-FLOW.md` §17.14),
while the stick pills and the checklist number every backup stick
from 2, the boot stick being 1 (§16): one stick was "Stick 1" on the
board and "Stick 3" on its pill. The top app bar showed where its back
arrow lands as the bare name of that place under the page's name
("Kind", "Tools"), which read as a subtitle of the page. Make codex32
shares' entry was titled by where it stands ("Share A · character 7 of
26") at the head of its card, which scrolls away under the bar while
the dice sheet and the worksheets below it are filled in.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-28).

- **One stick numbering.** The backup board, its ⋮ menu and its add
  sheets name each stick by its pill's number: the number the backup
  flow gives its stick where the flow has one for every stick of the
  board, else from 2 in the order the flow asks for them (each place's
  vaults' sticks, then its sticks of files, place by place, then the
  software's stick of files), across every place. The board's empty
  sticks take the numbers after them. No stick on the board reads
  Stick 1, the boot stick's number.
- **"Back to" under the page's name.** The top app bar's line under
  the page's name reads "Back to Kind", "Back to Tools", on every page
  with a way back and every layout (`faraday_ui::nav::app_bar`), the
  Starter's too; where the bar's tools leave no room for it whole (the
  Pi's panel off mainnet), the place's name alone, as far as it fits.
- **The codex32 entry's step in the bar.** While Make codex32 shares'
  Characters card is open and a character is still to come, the top
  app bar's title is where the entry stands, "Share A · character 7 of
  26", and stays in view as the page scrolls; where the bar has no room
  for it whole (the Pi's 2.8-inch panel) it reads "Share A · 7 of 26",
  and where it has room for neither (the Pi's panel off mainnet, its
  mode pill and eye beside the title) the bar keeps the flow's name and
  the card reads the step. Otherwise the card reads **Characters**.

**Amends.** `docs/BACKUP-FLOW.md` §17.14's "Stick 1" per place;
`docs/CODEX32.md` §6.4.1's title, now the bar's.

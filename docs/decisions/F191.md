# F191 The switches C1 to C14 before the timing fixes, and a switch lands with its cross-check change (2026-10-31)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** crypto
**Held by:** crypto.crosscheck-sources

**Why.** F178 ordered the work of `docs/ESTABLISHED.md` as the timing
fixes first, then the switches, then the rule in STANDARDS §0 and its
enforcement. A piece that moves to a library cannot be judged by that
library (F180 point 8), and the choice of each action's outside judges
(ESTABLISHED §9, F192) found the switches and timing fixes after which
a judge must be added or named: X.509 and DER, the finalizer, hex,
JSON, Adler-32, the zero check and Argon2id, the scalar arithmetic on
secrets, BIP-352's tweaks, codex32's arithmetic, the word lookups and
the one copy of each kept piece. Landed apart, a commit would carry a
piece judged by the library it moved to, or a judge dropped and not
replaced.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-31; `docs/ESTABLISHED.md` §8, §9.2). It
amends F178's order.

1. The order is ESTABLISHED's A0, A1, B1, X1, C1 to C14, then A4 to
   A7, then C15, then D1: the switches C1 to C14 before the timing
   fixes, the rule in STANDARDS §0 and its enforcement last, as before.
   A1 comes second, with X8, because it was written before this order
   was set (owner, 2026-11-02). C15 still comes after A4.
2. A switch or a timing fix whose action needs a cross-check change
   lands with it, in one commit and one passing `just crosscheck`. The
   change is the X batch ESTABLISHED §8 names in its row.

# F209 Check a signer carries the step card's touch-target exception (2026-11-04)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** design.touch-targets

**Why.** `tools/touch-targets.counts` (F152) only falls, and a screen new
to it is a new line. Check a signer (`Screen::SignerCheck`,
`docs/SIGNER-CHECK.md` §3, §4) has three step cards, and the head of each,
drawn by the shared `faraday/faraday-core/src/flow.rs`, reaches 44 to 49 px
against a floor of 51 at 1024 × 600, as on every flow (F200, F201). Nothing
else on the screen is under the floor.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-04). The counts gain `small_240x320 SignerCheck
SignerCheck 2`, `small_480x640 SignerCheck SignerCheck 3` and
`stick_1024x600 SignerCheck SignerCheck 3`: the new screen inherits the step
card's existing exception. Nothing else is raised. A fix of the card's head
at its source lowers these lines with every other flow's; it is not done
here because it moves the layout of every flow.

# F188 Touch targets on the 2.8-inch are 44 dp, Apple's minimum (2026-11-02)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** design.touch-targets

*Amends F152 (2026-11-02): the floor is 44 dp on the 2.8-inch's size class, 48 dp on every other layout. Amends F108 (2026-11-02): a press on the 2.8-inch is taken on at least 44 × 44 dp.*

**Why.** The 2.8-inch (Waveshare 2.8inch DPI LCD, 480 × 640 at
286 dpi) is 268 × 358 dp, the smallest screen Faraday runs on. Material
3's 48 dp floor takes a large share of it: its layouts hold the most
targets under the floor, and a screen of controls at 48 dp leaves little
room for what they act on. 44 × 44 pt is Apple's minimum touch target
(Human Interface Guidelines, Accessibility), held on every iPhone, so a
press of that size is one people already make every day.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-02).

- **44 dp on the 2.8-inch's size class**, the compact and short layout of
  `docs/IMAGES.md` §3 (268 × 358 dp), drawn in the tests at 480 × 640
  and 240 × 320. Every other layout keeps 48 dp.
- **The touch-target test holds 44 dp there**, and
  tools/touch-targets.counts is written again at that floor: its counts
  for those two sizes only fall.
- **A difference from Material**, recorded here as every one is: a target is
  44 dp where Material says 48 dp, on that size class only.
- **Built:** the touch target token by size class (`tokens::touch`), the
  test's floor, the counts, and the text of `docs/DESIGN.md`,
  `docs/LAYOUT.md` and `docs/M3-CHECKS.md`. The shared components and the
  compact bar take it from `Ui::touch`; a screen's own target sizes written
  as `TOUCH` stay 48 dp until moved to it.

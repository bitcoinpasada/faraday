# F200 Convert a seed carries the step-card head's touch-target exception every step flow carries (2026-11-04)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** design.touch-targets

**Why.** `tools/touch-targets.counts` (F152) only falls, and a screen
new to it is a new line. Convert a seed (`Screen::Convert`,
`docs/SEED-CONVERSION.md` §6) draws nothing under the floor of its own:
the targets under it are the head of each step card, drawn by the shared
`faraday/faraday-core/src/flow.rs`, as in BIP-85 and New key.

**Decision** (2026-11-04, the recommended answer, taken on the owner's
word to go on with it, and for the owner to confirm). The counts gain
`small_240x320 Convert Convert 1`, `small_480x640 Convert Convert 1` and
`stick_1024x600 Convert Convert 3`: the new screen inherits the step
card's existing exception. Nothing else is raised. A fix of the card's
head at its source would lower these lines with every other step flow's;
it was not made here because it changes the layout of every flow.

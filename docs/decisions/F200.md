# F200 Convert a seed carries the step-card head's and the keyboard's touch-target exceptions (2026-11-04)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Amended by F201
**Area:** design
**Held by:** design.touch-targets

*Amended by F201 (2026-11-04): `stick_1024x600 Convert Convert` is 4.*

**Why.** `tools/touch-targets.counts` (F152) only falls, and a screen
new to it is a new line. Convert a seed (`Screen::Convert`,
`docs/SEED-CONVERSION.md` §6) draws nothing under the floor of its own:
the targets under it are the head of each step card, drawn by the shared
`faraday/faraday-core/src/flow.rs`, as in BIP-85 and New key, and the keys
of the shared on-screen keyboard while Check my copy is typed, as in the
backup flow and Add a key.

**Decision** (2026-11-04, the recommended answer, taken on the owner's
word to go on with it, and for the owner to confirm). The counts gain
`small_240x320 Convert Convert 1`, `small_480x640 Convert Convert 1` and
`stick_1024x600 Convert Convert 3`, and `stick_1024x600 Convert Osk 38`:
the new screen inherits the step card's and the keyboard's existing
exceptions. Nothing else is raised. A fix of the card's
head or the keyboard at its source would lower these lines with every
other screen's; it was not made here because it changes the layout of
every flow.

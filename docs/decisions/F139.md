# F139 A compact top app bar's title steps down to Title M where Title L would be cut (2026-10-27)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design

**Why.** Material 3's small top app bar sets the page's name in Title L
and shortens it with an ellipsis where it does not fit. On the Pi's
268 dp panel the bar's actions (the ?, the mode pill, Cancel, the
keyboard button, the eye) leave the name 70 to 120 dp, and in Title L
a short name such as "At a glance" was cut to "At a gl…": the page's
name no longer read.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-27; DESIGN §4.1, `docs/LAYOUT.md` §8). A
top app bar's title (`faraday_ui::nav::app_bar`) is Title L where it
fits whole beside the bar's actions, and Title M where Title L would be
cut; it is shortened only where Title M is cut too, and then at a word,
its first word at least, with an ellipsis ("Make a new…"). Where not
even its first word would show whole, the name is left off the bar (a
fragment such as "Cre…" names nothing; the step's own heading names
the page).
The place Back goes stays Label M under it. The title ends where the
actions' own room begins (an icon's 48 dp target, the pill's gap,
Cancel's padding), with no second gap. This differs from Material's
small top app bar, whose title is always Title L.

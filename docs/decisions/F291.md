# F291 Home shows what is loaded, under its search bar and the first actions; the sidebar no longer lists keys and wallets (2026-11-19)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Amends F278 (2026-11-16): "The sidebar holds what is in progress, keys,
wallets and the sticks" becomes "The sidebar holds what is in progress and
the sticks". Amends F91 (2026-10-23) the same way: the drawer holds flows
in progress and the sticks, not keys and wallets. Every other line of both
holds.*

**Why.** Keys and wallets were shown in two places on the wide layout, the
sidebar's lists and Home's cards, and the three first actions vanished as
soon as anything was loaded, so the ways to make, restore or spend moved
somewhere else at the moment a person had started. With more on Home, its
search bar under everything was found last.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-19; `docs/HOME.md`, `docs/DESIGN.md` §5,
`docs/LAYOUT.md` §3). On the wide layout and the rail's windows:

1. **The search bar first.** Home's search bar is at the top of the page,
   centred and at most `LEAD_MAX` wide on the wide layout, full width on
   the rail's windows, and stays put while the page under it scrolls.
   While something is typed its results take the page: nothing else of
   Home is drawn until the search is cleared, as on the phone-size window.
2. **Then the order.** Under the bar: the lead, while a job waits; the
   three first actions (Make a new wallet, Restore an existing wallet,
   Spend from a backup), always, not only while nothing is loaded; a row of
   the loaded wallets beside the Backups tile, in one small style; then a
   Keys section in the same style, each key by its fingerprint and name
   (and its passphrase, when it has one), a press opening it in Explore.
3. **The sidebar.** Its Keys and Wallets lists go. It keeps its
   destinations, the rows of flows in progress (a flow in progress stays
   one press away, F41) and the sticks; vaults stay a count on the Vaults
   destination.
4. **Unchanged.** The phone-size window and the Pi's menu (their box of
   what is loaded and their order), what each press opens, and the
   destinations and their names.

**Against Material.** Nothing: a navigation drawer holds destinations
(and here the flows in progress), not the content of a page; Material 3's
search bar sits at the top of the page, and its search view covers the
content while a search is active.

Material pages: `https://m3.material.io/components/navigation-drawer/guidelines`,
`https://m3.material.io/components/search/guidelines`.

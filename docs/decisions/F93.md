# F93 Codex32 made by hand, any split (2026-10-22)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** keys

**Why.** The owner asked for full codex32 (BIP 93) support for making
shares, as the codex32 booklet does it, with every step shown so that a
person can follow it on paper. Until now Faraday read codex32 strings
and split a seed it held into two fixed schemes, and showed none of the
arithmetic. The plan is `docs/CODEX32.md`.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-22).

- **Make codex32 shares**, a flow of its own beside BIP-39 words at the
  top of New key's Start card: the person chooses the scheme, types a
  four-character identifier before any entropy, and puts in their own
  entropy (dice by the booklet's de-biasing worksheet, six-faced only;
  characters typed; or initial shares made by hand). The booklet's
  worksheets are on screen and fill as each roll and character
  arrives; every computed cell opens to what made it; after entry each
  worksheet is read back on its own tab (`docs/CODEX32.md` §6, §7).
- **Any split, everywhere**: a threshold of 2 to 9, or none (the secret
  alone), and up to 31 strings, in Make codex32 shares, in a backup's
  copies by hand and in the backup plan's codex32 form.
- **Lengths**: 48 characters (128 bits) by default; 74 (256 bits)
  offered; 127 (512 bits, the long checksum) only for a codex32 backup
  of a key made of BIP-39 words, never in Make codex32 shares.
- **What the person can check**: the entropy's characters stand
  unchanged in the data of the secret (no threshold) or of each initial
  share; every checksum row is shown; after any generation, a step to
  check the strings on another implementation.
- **Shares made by hand**, typed in: each checksum checked, the first
  failure named, the rest derived step by step; the secret is never
  typed whole. **Add a share** later from any k strings of a set.
  **Check a string** by hand without loading it.
- **The identifier**: typed in Make codex32 shares; from the master
  fingerprint where a seed already exists (a key's copies by hand, the
  plan); one already used by another set on the device or in an open
  vault is refused.
- **Templates**: the Starter's blank templates gain codex32 sheets and
  the booklet's published worksheets for 48 characters and its 256-bit
  checksum sheet; no sheet the booklet has not published is drawn. The
  booklet's MIT notice is kept in the repository and on the codex32
  Learn page, not printed on the sheets.
- **Masking**: none on Faraday Demo; on the stick and the Pi the
  live sheets are unmasked while the flow is open and masked when it is
  left, the eye masking them at any time.
- **A key that already has a codex32 set** places and checks that set
  in its backup and is never split again but by Rotate the set; its key
  record keeps the set's plain facts, no strings.
- Faraday's own randomness never makes a fresh codex32 seed, with the
  one exception of F96.

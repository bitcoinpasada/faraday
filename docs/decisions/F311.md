# F311 The navigation's badges are Bitcoin orange with a white count, in every theme (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Differs from Material 3, where a badge is error-coloured, and from F303, where it was warning-coloured.*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-21): the badges should match Bitcoin's symbol, bright orange with a white count.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21).

1. It replaces F303 point 2. The destination badges (Files, Wallets, Vaults; the count badge and the dot alike, in the rail, the bar and the expanded rail) are filled in #F7931A in every theme and contrast level, not harmonised to the theme and not the warning role, with the count's label in white (`faraday_ui::pal::BADGE`, `ON_BADGE`, fixed colours).
2. White on #F7931A reads about 2.3:1, under the contrast thresholds. This is the owner's own choice, matching the Bitcoin mark; no contrast check holds this pair and none is to change it.

# F260 The codex32 booklet's step-through and tracker leave the app (2026-11-14)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** keys
**Held by:** review

*Amends F93 (codex32 made by hand, any split): its Demo step-through of the computer's dice and the Working area's tracker go.*

**Why.** The step-through, its tour of the checksum and translation
worksheets and the tracker built on it are several thousand lines that
only re-show, a step at a time, arithmetic the live worksheets already
show cell by cell. Every computed cell still opens to its working, so a
person can still follow the booklet on paper.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-14). The walk (Faraday Demo's step-through of
the computer's five dice, with Previous, Next and Play), the tour and the
tracker (the Working area) are deleted, with their strings, tests and
the cryptographic action that described the computer reading the
booklet's dice. What stays: making shares with the person's own dice by
the de-biasing worksheet, characters typed, shares by hand, the live
worksheets, the worksheets read back, every cell opening to its working,
Add a share, Check a string, error correction, backups, Codex32QR,
templates, Practice's sample and Demo's one die elsewhere.

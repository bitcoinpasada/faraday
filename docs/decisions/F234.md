# F234 Work kept out of the app is held by reachability, not by a cargo feature (2026-11-10)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Amended by F257
**Area:** wallets
**Held by:** standards.out-of-app

*Amended by F257 (2026-11-14): silent-payment sending is removed from the repository, as FROST was, and no longer kept out of the app here; the rule holds for the rest.*

*Supersedes F109: the owner's rule of 2026-10-23 stands, with the cargo feature that switched it replaced by reachability.*

**Why.** F109 kept FROST out of the app behind one cargo feature,
`unreleased`. FROST was removed from the repository (F127) and the
feature went with it, so the rule had nothing left to describe its
switch. The rule itself stands and is held another way.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-23; restated 2026-11-10).

- **The rule.** A feature whose standard is unpublished, or whose
  construction is Faraday's own and unreviewed, stays out of the app
  until its standard is published or it is reviewed. Its code stays in
  the repository, built and tested, and is reached by no shipped code.
  `docs/STANDARDS.md` §0 states the rule and §0.1 lists what it leaves
  out.
- **How it is held.** `tools/lint-standards.py`, check 2: what is kept
  out (`[[out_of_app]]` in `tools/lint-standards.toml`, an action of
  `docs/crypto/actions.toml` whose roots are its functions, or the ids
  listed) is reached by no shipped function of a crate under `faraday/`,
  through calls in its crate and calls into other crates (the
  inventory's `callees` and `cross`, resolved as `check.py` resolves
  them). What no shipped code reaches is not linked into a shipped
  binary. An entry's `unnamed` is a pattern no line of a `faraday/`
  crate's `src/` may match. The check does not see a method call whose
  receiver's type the inventory cannot tell (`docs/CRYPTO-LEARN.md`
  §4.4).
- **MuSig2 signing across places is out of the app.** The app keeps no
  MuSig2 secret nonce past the session it was drawn in: a round that
  goes first holds its nonce in memory for that one transaction, and a
  lock or a power-off ends it. Rounds sealed into the vault across a
  lock are not offered. MuSig2 with the session kept open between the
  rounds, and signing last by BIP 327's `DeterministicSign`, stay.
- **FROST is removed from the repository** (F127), not kept out of the
  app.
- **Coming back** is the owner's decision, recorded in a decision that
  amends this one: work kept out returns when its standard is published
  or its construction has been reviewed; MuSig2 rounds across places when
  that construction has been reviewed.

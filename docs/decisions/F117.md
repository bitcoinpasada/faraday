# F117 Faraday Desktop matches the device (2026-10-25)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design

**Why.** Make codex32 shares on Faraday Desktop had no eye and never
masked its sheets (CODEX32 §1.1), a difference from the stick and the Pi
with no reason in the platform (owner, 2026-10-25).

**Decision.** Faraday Desktop matches the device. It differs only where
the platform forces it or an owner decision says so: F61 (no mainnet),
sticks as folders, Quit, Transfer, Make PDF, paste, the seed line and
the wordmark. The codex32 eye is the first case brought back: Faraday
Desktop shows the eye and masks and unmasks the sheets exactly as the
stick and the Pi (CODEX32 §6.4.6). Show as QR is offered on Faraday
Desktop beside Make PDF when the QR fits, as on the device.

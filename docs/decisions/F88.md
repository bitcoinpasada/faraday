# F88 Seed conversions are one tool, and only to forms that restore the same wallet (2026-10-23)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** keys
**Held by:** review

**Why.** Conversions between seed forms were drifting into vault items
and the backup board, and some of the easy-looking ones (BIP-39 entropy
written as SLIP-39 or codex32, words in another list) open a different,
empty wallet that looks right.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-23; the spec is
`docs/SEED-CONVERSION.md`, planned).

- One tool, Tools › Convert a seed, writes a key held here in another
  form; vault items and the backup board hold no conversions.
- A conversion is offered only where its result gives the same master
  fingerprint in its standard, checked against that standard's
  published vectors and another implementation, and read back on the
  device before it is shown. Each result names its standard and the
  software that restores it.
- The conversions that give another wallet are listed as not offered,
  with the reason: a seed to BIP-39 words, an xprv to a seed, BIP-39
  entropy as SLIP-39 or codex32, BIP-39 in another list, a 64-byte seed
  as SLIP-39. Shamir39 is not offered unless the owner decides
  otherwise.
- Set actions (add, replace, rotate) stay with the backup, for codex32
  and SLIP-39 alike.
- Shamir39 is not offered. A SLIP-39 result is 1 of 1 with the
  extendable flag set and iteration exponent 1, and never carries a
  SLIP-39 passphrase; a 20, 24 or 28-byte codex32 seed is not offered
  as SLIP-39.
- A master seed as hex and an xprv leave through `Reveal::PaperForm`.
  aezeed is checked against LND's published vectors only.

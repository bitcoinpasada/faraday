# F87 A backup's copies carry the date they were last checked, and a recovery path's wait is stated (2026-10-23)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** backup

**Why.** A plan says where each copy is, not whether it is still there
and readable. The dates Faraday kept were written by two presses only,
were never shown as an age, and a copy matched in the checklist left no
date at all. And a wallet with a timelocked recovery path lets its
recovery keys spend any coin left unmoved past the wait, which Faraday,
offline, cannot watch.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-23; the spec is
`docs/BACKUP-FLOW.md` §18, planned).

- Every check of a copy dates that copy in the plan's notes in the
  vault; a check from the checklist asks which place's copy it was
  where the plan keeps that form at more than one.
- A date is written only from a checked clock (`docs/TOTP.md` §1.3);
  with the clock not checked, dates are shown and no age or due mark is.
- The chart, its sheets, the card's strip and Backups show each copy's
  age; a copy is due past the check interval, proposed at 12 months.
- A wallet with a relative timelock states each path's wait in blocks
  and days on its card, the chart and the wallet sheet, with a margin
  to move coins within (proposed 90 % of the shortest wait), and the
  sheet carries a table of deposits to fill by hand.
- The interval is 12 months for every copy, one constant; for a wallet
  with a relative wait it is the shorter of 12 months and the margin,
  90 % of the shortest wait. A copy whose only check has no date is due
  once the clock is checked. Home gains no tile.
- A check matched with no vault open is held in RAM only until the next
  unlock, never in the kept state or on disk outside the vault.
- A recovery wallet's card offers **I moved every coin**, a dated vault
  note; the Starter's blank wallet sheet carries an empty Spend paths
  block.

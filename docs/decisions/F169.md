# F169 A GPG key is restored from its paperkey and its public certificate (2026-10-29)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** crypto
**Held by:** standards.refusals-stay, crypto.tested

**Why.** A GPG key made in Faraday cannot be made again from seed
words. Its paperkey is shown so that it can be written down (F32), but
only GnuPG could turn it back into a key: a person who lost the key's
vault had no way to bring it back into Faraday.

**Decision** (the owner's ask, 2026-10-15, and answers, 2026-10-23; the
reference is `docs/GPG-PAPERKEY.md`).

- A GPG key is restored from its paperkey's lines and its public
  certificate, through the backup flow (F31), as a wallet is restored:
  the plan (two places or more), the sticks, the vaults, then the
  paperkey at step 4, typed line by line or scanned as a code. The
  certificate comes in as a public file (F22), ASCII armour only, or
  as a scanned code, filed in Files as `{name}-{fp8}.asc`.
- The lines are paperkey's base16 form, each checked by its CRC-24 as
  it is ended, and the whole data by its own; a code is paperkey's
  base16 lines or its raw form, every line checked the same way. The
  line format and the refusals' words are paperkey 1.6's.
- The result is the type 7 record Make wrote: the certificate and the
  kept revocation are made again from the seeds and the times read from
  the certificate, and every signature of the key's in the certificate
  must be the one Faraday makes, byte for byte.
- Faraday refuses what paperkey would take and Faraday cannot verify:
  a key of another algorithm or shape, a key made elsewhere whose
  signatures are not Faraday's, a secret protected by a passphrase, a
  secret that does not make the certificate's public key, a certificate
  with no user ID, and a CRC that does not match (paperkey's
  `--ignore-crc-error` has no counterpart). Signatures by other keys
  are set aside and counted, not kept. No correction is suggested for a
  mistyped line, and no paperkey file is read from a stick.
- The restored key's bytes are held by `faraday-keys` from each line
  checked, and the key enters the vaults as a made key does. No reveal
  is added to F70's list.
- A restored key signs at once, as a restored wallet's keys do. In
  Practice the restore is dimmed, as Make is.

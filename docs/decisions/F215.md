# F215 A GPG key's paperkey has a code, for moving the key to a vault on another machine (2026-11-05)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** QR
**Held by:** keys.paperkey-one-code, speed.shown-secret-held

*Amends F28: its rule that a secret gets no QR gains a third exception, and `docs/QR.md` §7.1 row 23 no longer covers the paperkey.*

**Why.** The owner's ask: a GPG key in the vault gets a way to move to
a new vault by QR. When the vault format changed, the key's paperkey
was typed into the new vault by hand, seven lines of hex digits. A GPG
restore's Scan the paperkey already reads a paperkey as a code, and
nothing in Faraday showed one. F28 gave the paperkey no code, its
reason "Paper, or never leave the vault".

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-05, who left the choice between the
private key and the paperkey open; the spec is `docs/GPG-PAPERKEY.md`
§12 and `docs/QR.md` §7.1).

- **The paperkey, not the private key.** The paperkey's only other way
  into a vault is typing, and the restore proves every byte of the
  record it makes (`docs/GPG-PAPERKEY.md` §1, §4.3). The certificate is
  public and already has its code. A GPG private key as a file or a code
  and a Secure Boot key stay as they are: no code.
- **As an entry's TOTP secret is shown** (F28, F123): **Show as QR**
  under the masked paperkey on the key's page, whose press opens the QR
  sheet with no hold; the sheet marked Secret; no PNG, no Outbox file.
- **Once, and held** (F195): the text is made at the press through the
  reveal the eye uses, `OpenVaults::gpg_paperkey` under
  `Reveal::GpgPaperkey`; no new reveal (F70). The view holds it and its
  code, wiped when the sheet closes, on leaving and at a lock or the
  idle lock. Drawing works nothing out.
- **The code holds the text the eye shows**: paperkey's base16 lines,
  seven for a key of Faraday's, at most 447 bytes, one plain-text code
  at version 16 and error correction M, 81 modules. Faraday's reader
  takes it, and so does the paperkey program from a file holding what
  any QR reader gives. The raw form (124 bytes, version 8, 49 modules)
  is not shown: nothing in the sender writes binary, it would need a
  second way of making the paperkey, and a tool that reads QR codes as
  text does not carry it.
- **One static code, with no choice of format.** The QR sheet offers
  neither UR, BBQr and Parts, nor a speed, nor a part size, for this
  view. A paperkey in parts is not read: Scan the paperkey takes whole
  codes, and the other scanners name a paperkey only when one frame
  holds it whole, so a paperkey in parts could pass one of them unnamed.
- **On the smallest panel the code takes the sheet's whole width**, with
  Done under it and scrolling where it does not fit, so that it is two
  pixels a module at 240 × 320 and four at 480 × 640, where the sheet as
  it stood gave one and three.
- **The move is the restore, unchanged.** The new vault takes the key
  through Restore a GPG key (F169, F31): the plan of two places or more,
  the sticks, the vaults, then the code at step 4. No shorter path skips
  the plan: F31's two places are the check on a key that cannot be made
  again, and Faraday cannot know the old vault will be kept. Copying an
  item between vaults open on one machine is another feature; this one
  is for two machines with only a camera between them.

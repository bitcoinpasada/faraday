# F201 Convert a seed's limit of step titles under the touch floor on the stick is 4 (2026-11-04)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** design.touch-targets

*Amends F200 (2026-11-04): `stick_1024x600 Convert Convert` is 4, not 3.*

**Why.** Convert a seed gained a fourth step, Settings. At 1024 × 600 the
title of a paged flow's open step, drawn by the shared
`faraday/faraday-core/src/flow.rs`, reaches 49 px against a floor of 51,
on every flow, so a fourth step is a fourth title under it. Convert's own
steppers and the room under Settings' title are spaced to the floor, so
nothing else on the screen is added to the count.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-04). `stick_1024x600 Convert Convert` goes
from 3 to 4. Nothing else is raised. The titles are fixed later at their
source, in the flow, which lowers this line with every other flow's; it
was not done here because it moves the layout of every flow.

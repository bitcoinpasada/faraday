# F144 With no one holding an earlier build's files, the formats drop the names before Faraday's, and another program's PSBT records are signed past (2026-10-28)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** wallets

**Why.** The byte strings that took part in a format or a derivation
kept their names from before Faraday (F72; `docs/STRUCTURE-PASS.md`
§3.2), and two refusals stood for files an earlier build could have left
with a person: the PSBT records of the removed threshold signing (F127)
and the sticks made before the partitions were renamed (F67). No one
holds such a file: Faraday has no users yet. The names and the refusals
stood for nothing.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-28).

- **The names are Faraday's.** The sealed secrets' labels
  (`faraday-sealed-v1`, `faraday-sealed-nonce`,
  `faraday-session-key-weak`), the signing context's blind
  (`faraday-secp-blind`), the Schnorr auxiliary randomness
  (`faraday-schnorr-aux`), the MuSig2 nonce tags (`Faraday/musig-rand`,
  `Faraday/musig-next-seed`), the KDBX entries' UUIDs
  (`faraday-kdbx-uuid`), the silent payments record's header
  (`faraday-silent 1`) and the baked outlines' magic (`FDOL`). A file of
  an earlier build in any of these formats is not read.
- **Another program's proprietary PSBT records are passed over**, as
  BIP 174 has a signer do with fields it does not know: an input
  carrying them is reviewed and signed as any other, and they stay as
  they were. The `UnreadRecords` warning, F127's block on records under
  the identifier `osk`, is gone.
- **A stick of the earlier layout** (`OSKBOOT` and `OSKDATA`) is refused
  as any disk that is not a Faraday stick is; nothing names it.

**Amends.** F127: its block on `osk` PSBT records. F136: `UnreadRecords`
leaves its list of blocked warnings.

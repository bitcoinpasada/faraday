# F314 Home's top row: the search bar and three tonal buttons on one 48 dp row, the box at its width (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Amended by F323 point 4 (owner, 2026-11-22), built: on the wide layout the search stands in the top app bar and Home's three buttons beside it are text-only pills, the field shrinking to fit, with no second row and no place level with the sidebar's Home; the 48 dp height stays. Home's card starts where every menu page's content does.*

*Differs from Material 3, where a search bar is 56 dp tall.* Checked against Material's Search (https://m3.material.io/components/search/specs) and Buttons (https://m3.material.io/components/buttons/guidelines): the buttons are filled tonal with an icon and a label; the search bar keeps its full round shape.

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-21): the three 84 dp tiles under the search bar were more than three ways in need, and the bar, the buttons and the sidebar's selected item should read as one line.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21).

1. Home's top row is the search bar and, after it, three tonal buttons, "New wallet", "Restore" and "Spend from backup", each with its icon, all as tall as the bar. The bar takes what the buttons leave. The row is the page's width, centred in the area beside the navigation, and stands where the sidebar's highlight of Home stands: bar, buttons and highlight are 48 dp tall and share top and bottom.
2. A window too narrow for the row puts the buttons on a second row under the bar, in three equal columns. The row never scrolls; only the box under it does (F313). The one-column layouts and the Pi's menu stay as they are.
3. The box of what is loaded is as wide as the row, so every edge lines up. This amends F303's box width (as wide as the search bar).
4. `SEARCH_BAR_H` is 48 dp, below Material's 56 dp, the owner's choice; Tools' search bar takes it too.

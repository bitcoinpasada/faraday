# F110 Faraday Desktop shows its screens at another screen's size (2026-10-24)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Amended by F118
**Area:** design

Faraday Desktop shows its screens at any of the layout's screen sizes
(`docs/PREVIEW-SIZE.md`), on the owner's ask (2026-10-23).

- **In Settings**, as a Preview size card after Display scale: Material
  3 puts an app-wide preference in Settings and keeps the top app bar
  for the screen's own actions; Settings is reached at every size, so
  the way back is always there.
- **The shell's display, not the screens.** The desktop shell gives
  the app a display of the chosen size in dp at the window's density
  and draws the frame centred, scaled down only to fit; the pointer is
  carried into it. Every layout rule (class, short, low, navigation,
  the keyboard rising on a small panel) follows as on that screen,
  with no screen special-cased.
- **Desktop only.** Offered by the shell (`Event::PreviewOffered`);
  the stick, the Pi, the Starter and `--panel` offer none.
- **Not kept.** The size lasts the launch, across locks; it is in no
  settings file, as nothing about it changes what the device does.

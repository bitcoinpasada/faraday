# F92 Faraday is laid out to Material 3 at every window size, from the Pi to a full monitor (2026-10-23)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design

**Why.** Faraday has two layouts: the small panel and a 1280 × 800 page
scaled to fit the window. Every size between them gets the small panel
stretched (a 7-inch panel, a half-screen window), a monitor gets the
1280 page larger rather than more room, and tiles and buttons stretch
to their column. Material 3 publishes tested rules for laying an app
out at each size.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-23: follow Material 3, make the screens
easy at each size, and have a full monitor get what Material gives it;
the design calls left to the spec, `docs/LAYOUT.md`, planned).

- Material 3's five window size classes, by width (compact, medium,
  expanded, large, extra-large), with short and low windows by height;
  each laid out at its own width, never scaled to fit.
- Navigation by class: the Pi panel keeps its Home menu; other compact
  windows a bar of five destinations; medium, expanded and low windows
  a rail; large and extra-large today's sidebar as Material's expanded
  rail.
- Home as a grid of tiles that gains columns; list and detail side by
  side from expanded up; Learn beside the page on extra-large.
- Nothing stretches: buttons as wide as their labels, cards in grids
  with a minimum column width, text at a readable measure.
- Laid out at the screen's density. When built, this supersedes F78.
- The look stays Faraday's own; the Starter follows the same rules
  (F91).

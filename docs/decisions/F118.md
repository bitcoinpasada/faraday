# F118 Faraday Desktop is a simulator: what is Desktop or Demo stands in a host strip outside the app (2026-10-25)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design

**Why.** On Faraday Desktop the stick pills, Load samples and the "Demo
only" card stood inside the app's own screen, and on a preview size
they were crammed inside the device's frame (owner, 2026-10-25), so it
was not clear what is the app and what is Desktop or Demo.

**Decision.** Faraday Desktop holds the app in a host
(`docs/DESKTOP-HOST.md`). The app area is laid out exactly as the device
of that size (F117): the window less a strip at its foot, or a preview
size's frame centred in the dark space above the strip. The strip, in
the host's own look and never inside the frame, holds the stick pills,
Samples, the preview size and every demo aid of the screen on show
(`docs/DEMO.md` §14's whole list, the computer's dice roller and the
codex32 walk included). The mode pill and the ? stay in the app. Demo
stays a mode of the app; only its aids move.

**Amends.** F110: the preview size leaves Settings for the strip's Size
menu, and the host, not the desktop shell, frames the preview. DEMO §14
and LAYOUT §6's "Demo only" card and §3's top-bar stick pills on Faraday
Desktop are replaced by the strip.

# F124 Randomness is never collected passively; pointer and keys are a source one picks (2026-10-26)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** security

**Why.** F86 took pointer movement and key timings on the desktop while
Add randomness was open, and left open whether to gather them from all
on-screen input. The owner answered (`docs/OWN-RANDOMNESS.md` §5 item 4):
nothing is gathered that the person did not put in for it.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-26; `docs/OWN-RANDOMNESS.md` §2.1, §2.2,
§4 test 6). Faraday never collects randomness passively. Pointer and
keys is a source the person picks on Add randomness or New key's
Generator card, on the desktop app; only pointer moves over its area
and keys typed while it is the chosen source are taken. Nothing is taken
in the background, from another source of the page, or from any field,
a passphrase included. Its events are committed every 64, when the
pointer leaves the area or a key is not its own, when Make the words is
pressed (the key then waits for fresh bytes), and when the source or
page is left. Add randomness and the Generator card open with no source
chosen and nothing running, Camera listed first, the camera off until
it is pressed.

This amends F86.

# F307 The top app bar names the page on every desktop window, back only below a destination, and the guide is the ? (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Amends F304 (2026-11-21): the top app bar's trailing actions; the ? is also the guide's only way in on the sidebar and rail windows.*

**Why.** The rail windows hid where you are: the top bar spent itself on the wordmark and carried no page title. On a destination's top page the way back led nowhere a person needs (the page the sidebar or rail already lists), and the guide stood twice, as a row in the navigation and as the ? in the bar. Material's top app bar carries the page title, and its up arrow belongs on a page below the top level.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21).

1. On every window with a top app bar beside a rail or the sidebar, the bar shows the page's title, Home included. The wordmark is the sidebar's head's alone; the rail carries only the shield mark at its top.
2. The way back is kept on pages one level below a destination (a wallet's detail, an open tool, a flow's step, an open Settings section) and removed from a destination's top page (Home, Check, Wallets, Files, Vaults, Tools, Settings, Transfer), on every window that has the bar beside a rail or the sidebar. The Pi's menu and the phone-size windows keep their bars as they were.
3. Guide leaves the sidebar and the rail. The ? in the top app bar is the way into the guide: it opens the section for the current page, and the guide's contents lead to the whole guide. The Pi's menu keeps its Guide row, as its screen has no top bar over Home.

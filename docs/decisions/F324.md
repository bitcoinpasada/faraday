# F324 A flow's pair is one card with an inset divider (2026-11-22)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** review

*Built for a flow whose card is a column (Your choices at the left, one card with its outline, the divider inset, the pair level); not built for wide content and the backup board. `docs/WORKSPACE.md`'s Open line says what is left.*

*Differs from Material 3, whose panes side by side are separate and stand a spacer apart (Canonical layouts, Supporting pane: https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/canonical-layouts/supporting-pane): here the two sides of a flow are one surface with a divider in the spacer's place. F331 (not built) would amend point 1: level 2 and a fill, the outline only at High contrast.*

**Why.** The pick or Your choices and the step are one piece of work: one surface shows that Your choices is what the step has done and goes back to.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-22).

1. In a flow the session pane (the pick, then Your choices) and the step card are one card. One thin rounded outline (OutlineVariant, no fill; the page stays one colour) goes round the two together, and the divider stands at the middle of the gap two cards would have, inset at its top and bottom by the card's padding.
2. The outer edges, and every row and heading inside, stay exactly where two cards would have them. The surface joins at the flow's entry, animated by F318's style and speed.
3. A menu page keeps two separate panes: they are two different things.
4. The backup board, a step of its flow, is one card in the same way.

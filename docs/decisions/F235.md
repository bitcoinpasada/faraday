# F235 The stick is shown in Files, and the stick visit is no destination (2026-11-10)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** stick
**Held by:** tests

*Amends F216 (the Pi's "Visit" in the top bar) and LAYOUT §3.*

**Why.** The stick visit was a screen of its own that the app opened when
a stick arrived and nothing was held. Its left column was Files' own two
lists again with checkboxes, and its right column was the stick. It joined
the rail after Tools, took Tools' place on the compact bar, and came and
went with the stick. Material 3's Navigation rail and Navigation bar take
top-level destinations that are distinct and the same throughout the app;
a temporary state of a device is not one, and half of this one was Files.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-10). *The stick is shown in Files, and the
stick visit is no destination.* A stick open to Faraday (attached, nothing
held) is a pane of Files: the stick's files beside Files' own lists from
expanded windows up, and Material's primary tabs **In Faraday · On
{stick}** below that. Files' own rows take a checkbox for writing while it
is open, so each file is listed once. A stick's arrival opens Files on
that pane, and pulling it goes where the pane's chip says: "Then: Unlock"
and "Then: {flow}" as before, and where the chip would have said "Then:
Home" the person stays on Files, the chip and the pane go. The rail and
the bar keep five fixed destinations, so Tools never gives way on the bar.
Files' badge is the Inbox count, or the small dot while a stick waits
unvisited. Home has no Stick visit tile (nor the Pi's Home menu a Card or
stick button); its lead tile reads "Files on {stick}" while a stick waits
unvisited. The disk process's refusal of a stick while anything is held is
unchanged: Files then shows "Lock to use {stick}" and no stick pane. "A
stick visit" stays the word for the act.

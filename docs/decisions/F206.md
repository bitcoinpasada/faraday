# F206 A KeePass file keeps its entry history and recycle bin, written back and never shown (2026-11-04)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** security
**Held by:** review

*Amends F190 (2026-11-02): point 3 dropped entry history and the recycle bin at the seal and named them at the unlock; they are kept.*

**Why.** A file a person keeps in a KeePass app is theirs: Faraday
changes only what the person changes in Faraday. Dropping the history and
the bin at the first seal destroyed what the person had kept there, and
the dialog that named them asked the person to agree to it.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-03). Entry history and the recycle bin are
kept as the bytes they came in, with their protected values in wiping
memory, and written back; `RecycleBinUUID` and `RecycleBinEnabled` are
written as read. They count toward the vault's size. Faraday has no
action that removes them.

- Nothing in either is shown, searched, counted or loaded: they are not
  groups or entries of the reader's model, so a key in the bin or in a
  history is not a key of the vault.
- Faraday's edits add no history. An entry deleted in Faraday goes with
  its own history, and nothing is moved to the bin.
- **Open this copy?** no longer names them; a file holding only history
  or a bin opens without the dialog. The seal count's reasons stay.
- An ambiguous `RecycleBinUUID` (two, or not 16 bytes) still marks
  `Meta` unreadable and its edits refuse; with two, both groups they
  name stay out of the model.

`docs/VAULT.md` §7.1 states it.

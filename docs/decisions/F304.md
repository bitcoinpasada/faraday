# F304 Lock, Restart and Power off are one power button at the top right of the top app bar (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Replaces where the sidebar's foot, the rail's foot and the bar's Lock icon put Lock and Power off. The Pi's menu keeps its rows.*

**Why.** The sidebar's foot was busy: a divider, then Lock and Power off as two labelled buttons, with the idle lock's pill under them. The power actions belong in one familiar place, the top right, as an operating system puts them.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21).

1. The top app bar's trailing actions carry a power icon button beside the ?, on every window that has the bar over its page. The sidebar's foot (the divider, Lock and Power off), the rail's foot and the compact bar's Lock icon go. The idle lock's pill stays at the sidebar's foot.
2. A press opens Material's menu anchored to the button: **Lock** (only while something is held, as the Lock button showed), **Restart** (where the computer offers it) and **Power off**, in that order, each with its icon. In Faraday Demo: **Lock** and **Quit**; it has no Restart.
3. Lock does what the Lock button did. Restart and Power off open the existing power sheet, which still offers both Restart and Power off whichever item was pressed: its holds and its list of what is kept and wiped are unchanged.
4. The Pi's menu (the 480 x 640 panel, no top app bar) keeps its Lock and Power off rows, and its ⋮ on a page keeps them. Keyboard reach is unchanged.

**Cost accepted.** Lock takes two presses where it took one. The idle lock is unchanged.

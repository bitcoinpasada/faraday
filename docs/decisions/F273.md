# F273 A costly press runs in the press or as a job on the worker; nothing is drawn first (2026-11-16)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** speed
**Held by:** tests

*Amends F184 (owner, 2026-11-16): its point 1's work under the line runs
in the press itself, with no frame drawn first and no tick after. Points 2
to 5 hold as written.*

**Why.** Since F184 a costly press takes one of two paths: over the line
it is a job on the worker, under it a frame is drawn first and the work
runs on the tick after (`docs/SPEED.md` §9 point 2). The second path is
for work a person would not see the screen stand still for, yet it keeps
machinery of its own: a press left waiting, a toast drawn before the
work, a tick that runs it, and a slider rule for the shares' files. Work
under a tenth of a second needs none of it, and work over it is the
worker's. One path for short work, the press itself, does what two did.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-16; `docs/WORKERS.md` §2.1).

1. **Under the line, in the press.** A costly press whose work takes at
   most 100 ms on a Raspberry Pi 3B+ at its full clock runs in the press,
   on the main thread, as any other press. No working frame and no
   working toast is drawn: the frame after the press shows its result, at
   most a tenth of a second later. A toast the work says of its own (a
   file saved, a key added) shows as it does now.
2. **Over the line, a job.** A press whose work takes longer is a job on
   the worker with its busy screen (`docs/WORKERS.md` §3, §4), and a
   vault's key stretch is a job whatever it measures (F184 point 2).
   Every press that is a job today stays one.
3. **Until a Pi measures it**, a release build on a desktop PC stands in
   at a tenth of the line, 10 ms, as F184 point 3 says: under it the
   press runs in the press, over it the press is a job, and a press whose
   timings fall either side of 10 ms from run to run is a job. A press
   not yet timed at all is timed so before it leaves the drawn-first
   path. The dev card's `work` rows read on a Pi 3B+ then confirm a press
   or move it, one commit for each move that says what was measured on
   what.
4. **The list is `docs/WORKERS.md` §2.1** and `slow.rs`'s lists made the
   same way; this decision names no press. The debug guard stays
   (`slow::guard`): a press that stretches a key or makes more than one
   picture is on the list of jobs or the list of presses run in the
   press, or it panics in every test, naming itself.
5. **A slider that changes the shares** (`BOmit`) sets its value at once
   and makes the files once it is let go, now by starting their job then.
6. **What goes when it is built.** The drawn-first path: `drawn_first`
   (the list of presses under the line stays, named for what it now
   holds), `Later::Press`, `working_text` and the working toast it sets,
   and the press half of `later_tick` and `later_now`. `Later::Vault`
   stays (a vault's stretch is still left for the frame that says it is
   under way), and with it the tick that starts it. `Later::Key` is not
   this decision's; it goes with the Lightning code that makes it.

**Still to do.** The Pi 3B+ timings are not yet taken: the dev card's
`work` rows read on a Pi 3B+ confirm each press that runs in the press
or move it to a job.

**What it costs.** A press near the line on the Pi holds the screen for
up to a tenth of a second with nothing drawn to say so; and until a Pi's
rows are read, a press the stand-in puts on the wrong side either holds
the screen too long or carries a job's machinery it does not need.

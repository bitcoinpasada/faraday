# F318 Motion: two settings, a style (Smooth motion, Crossfade, Off) and a speed (600 ms by default), that never change on their own; the session pane glides, the rest fades through (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Not built. `docs/WORKSPACE.md`'s Open line says what is left.*

*Follows Material 3's motion (Transitions: container transform and fade through; Easing and duration: the emphasized curve and the duration tokens, 600 ms being long 4). Differs from Material in one named point, a deliberate difference approved by the owner (2026-11-21): a step inside a flow takes the page's 600 ms, past Material's 450 ms fade through and its rule that duration grows with the area moved, which F296's 300 ms followed. Amends F296 (the step fade's fixed 300 ms), F108 as MOTION.md §3.5 applies it (Reduce motion becomes the style's Off) and F222 (its HDMI default, replaced by point 4's measured one).*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-21): today's 200 ms linear crossfade is too quick for a change where much moves, and the pane, which reads as a window, should move to its place rather than jump. Motion must never change by itself: a person sees the same motion every time, as they set it.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21).

1. **The layout never depends on motion.** The pane's place (F316) and Your choices at the left (F319) are the same with every style; with Off every screen is where the motion would have ended, at once.
2. **Style is a setting:** "Smooth motion" (the session pane glides between pages as Material's container transform, the rest fades through), "Crossfade" (today's blend of two kept screens) and "Off" (at once). Reduce motion (MOTION.md §3.5) folds into Off: one setting, never two. A kept `motion=reduced` reads as Off and `motion=full` as Smooth motion.
3. **Speed is a second setting:** Quick (250 ms), Medium (400 ms) and Relaxed (600 ms, the default), on Material's emphasized curve, for Smooth motion and Crossfade. Every page change and every step inside a flow takes it (in place of F296's 300 ms). Two settings rather than one: style and speed together are seven values, more than a segmented button holds.
4. **Motion never changes on its own.** No machine steps down from one style to another while it runs, and no transition picks its own style. Desktops default to Smooth motion at 600 ms; the Pi's default style and speed are set once, for its panel and for its HDMI screen each, from measurements made ahead of time (`docs/SPEED.md` §7: Smooth motion if the glide's steps keep to the panel's frame, else Crossfade if the blend's do, else Off). After that only the person changes them, in Settings, whose lines say what each style does. Today nothing turns motion off at run time on a slow machine (the gate was deleted on 2026-11-16, `docs/SPEED.md` §12); that stays so. F222's default of Reduce motion on a Pi's HDMI screen, the one default off by what the machine finds, goes (owner, 2026-11-21): the HDMI screen keeps a default of its own, chosen ahead of time from measurements at 1920 × 1080 and at 1280 × 800 (WXGA, which the owner shows centred on a larger screen), never a step down while running. The owner measures all of the Pi's timings (the panel, both HDMI sizes, the bottom sheets) with the first test build, in one sitting.
5. **What moves, under Smooth motion:** a row arriving in the session pane fades in while its space opens, and one leaving fades out as the rows under it slide up; a list expanding in place changes height smoothly; a key added in a signing flow arrives as its row, its tonal emphasis easing away over the speed's duration as Sign's button fades on. Under Crossfade each of these blends; under Off each is at once.
6. **Hover** fades its state layer in over 150 ms and out over 200 ms, fixed, not scaled by the speed, at once under Off. It is the only hover and press motion: no ripple, no lift, nothing that moves.
7. **Secrets.** A frame that showed a secret keeps no image to fade from: such a change is at once, as now. A step that shows or takes a secret fades drawn live (F296 point 2); at 600 ms its fade out, after which its secret state is released, lasts about twice today's 48 ms. The owner said yes to the animation in principle (2026-11-21), the window being about 96 ms and reading a secret in it needing code or memory access on the device, both outside the security model; it is a security trade-off. The owner said yes to building it (2026-11-22): it is built and tested on its own, and its results are shown to the owner before it reaches main.
8. **Bottom sheets slide up** (the Pi and small windows) at the speed under Smooth motion, blend under Crossfade and are at once under Off, if the Pi keeps up (timed with the first test build). **Toasts, the chart's side sheet and the theme fade** follow the style (they stop at Off) and keep their own durations, 200, 400 and 350 ms.

# F318 Motion: the This session card glides between pages, the rest fades through, and one Motion setting sets every menu change's duration (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Proposed
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Not built. `docs/WORKSPACE.md`'s Open line says what is left.*

*Follows Material 3's motion (Transitions: container transform and fade through; Easing and duration: the emphasized curve and the duration tokens). Would amend F296 (the step fade's fixed 300 ms) and F222 (its HDMI default).*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-21): today's 200 ms linear crossfade is too quick for a change where much moves, and the card, which reads as a window, should move to its place rather than jump.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21).

1. From Home to a menu page the This session card moves and resizes to its new place (container transform); the rest of the page fades through. A frame that showed a secret keeps no image to fade from: such a change is at once, as now.
2. Motion is a setting on Settings › Appearance: Off, Quick (about 250 ms), Standard (the default, about 400 ms; 400 or 450 ms is the owner's pick from renders) and Relaxed (about 600 ms), on Material's emphasized curve. Every menu change takes it; a step inside a flow takes three quarters of it.
3. Off replaces the Reduce motion switch and stops all it stops. A kept `motion=reduced` reads as Off and `motion=full` as Standard; F222's default on a Pi's HDMI screen becomes Off until the person chooses.
4. On the Pi the glide is timed as a late-flow frame (`docs/SPEED.md` §7) before it is built there; where it costs too much the Pi keeps a plain fade of the kept frames, and may cap the duration.

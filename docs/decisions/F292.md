# F292 A control Practice refuses answers a press with one toast (2026-11-19)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Amends F81: an action Practice refuses still makes nothing, and a press on
it now says why. Amends `docs/DEMO.md` §7 ("a press does nothing").*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-19, from Practice's Derive with BIP-85, Value
step): a greyed control that does nothing when pressed reads as broken.
Some refused controls (BIP-85's eye, the node key's Show, the scan key and
descriptor outputs) were not even greyed, and silently did nothing. Two
refusals had texts of their own (Sweep's toast, Convert's reason line), so
the same condition was said three ways.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-19; `docs/DEMO.md` §4, §7).

- **One answer.** A press on any action Practice refuses (`demo_refuses`)
  shows one toast, "Not available in Practice: this is an online
  computer", one string in `faraday-strings`, and does nothing else: no
  sheet, no lead, no reveal, nothing saved or shown.
- **Drawn greyed, every one.** Each refused control is drawn in DESIGN
  §4.11's dimmed state, the eyes and Show buttons included, with no reason
  line on the screen.
- **The difference from Material 3.** Material's disabled control takes no
  input. These keep Material's disabled look (no hover, focus or pressed
  state layer, not in the keyboard's focus order) but take a pointer or
  touch press, only to show the toast. Every other dimmed control in every
  app still takes no press: the exception is the actions Practice refuses,
  named so by the app, not dimmed controls in general.
- **The other texts become the one string.** Sweep's own toast ("Practice
  sweeps no private key") is replaced by it, and Convert's Set source joins
  the refused actions: its "Not offered in Practice" line goes and a press
  shows the toast.
- **Unchanged.** What Practice refuses (DEMO §4) and where the reason is
  explained (the Learn page, DEMO §7) stay as they are; a row Practice
  leaves out (the Seed XOR "by hand" row of the glance sheet) stays out.

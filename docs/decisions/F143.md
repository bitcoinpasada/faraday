# F143 The user guide's key is a Practice sample, and New key keeps a sample's own words (2026-10-27)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design

**Why.** The user guide's walkthroughs follow one key, `3dca6805`, made
in `docs/guide/walk/new-key.md` from EntropyLab's published dice
transcript, and then typed back, backed up and used in wallets beside
the samples zebra and summer. Practice took only the samples, and New
key in Practice put a handout in place of whatever the rolls made, so a
reader could not follow the guide in Faraday Demo's default mode and was
sent to Testnet for it.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-27; `docs/MODES.md` §5.1, §7).

- **The guide's key is a sample.** Its words are those
  `tools/vectors/entropy/dice.json`'s words vector gives (the
  transcript's words, the last completed by its checksum), public with
  no passphrase and with `faraday` as every BIP-39 sample is. Load
  samples lists it first among the Keys, named **Guide key · 3dca6805**;
  it is not a handout.
- **New key keeps words that are a sample's own.** At Lock in, words
  made that are a sample's, word for word, are kept as made, and the Key
  card says "These words are sample NAME" in place of naming a handout.
  Any other words are replaced by the next handout as before. Nothing
  looks at the rolls: the words made are compared with the samples'
  words, and the key store still takes only fingerprints of the public
  set, so a key that is no sample is refused as before.
- **Amends** F81 (New key's result is replaced by a handout unless it is
  a sample's own words; the samples include the guide's key).

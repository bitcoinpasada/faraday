# F258 The impact registry keeps only the pairs and what reads them; review rules are a checklist (2026-11-14)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** repository
**Held by:** review

*Replaces F151 (a review rule a change triggers is attested by a `Reviewed:` trailer). Amends F149: points 3, 4 and 6 are narrowed as below; points 1, 2 and 5 hold for the rules that stay.*

**Why.** Of the registry's rules, about half were review rules and over a
third were held rules. A review rule was met by a `Reviewed:` line, and
the check could only see that the line was there: over six thousand were
written in under three weeks without the check telling a read one from an unread
one. A held rule repeated what a lint already holds and fails on. The
list of checks generated from all of them was rewritten at almost every
change. And with no range given, `impact.py check` compares the tip with
its merge base on `main`, so on a push to `main` it evaluated nothing.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-14).

1. **What stays.** `tools/impact.toml` keeps its pair rules, their
   `lines` patterns tightened so that they fire only on the change they
   are about, the forbid rule that a decision or a document is moved to
   `docs/history/` and never deleted, and the consumers (the apps and the
   website, whose blocking pairs the website's publish reads).
   `tools/impact.py` keeps `plan` and `check`, the pair logic and the
   consumers; `Impact-skip: <rule>: <reason>` still waives a pair.
2. **What goes.** Every review rule, every held rule and every check
   entry, the trailer rule for a `Material:` line, the `Reviewed:`
   trailer and its handling, the generated list of checks and the script's
   commands that wrote it, and a pair another lint already holds. A
   decision's `**Held by:**` line is no longer checked against the
   registry.
3. **Review rules become a checklist.** Their one-line titles are a
   checklist in the reviewer's own file and in the area documents
   `CLAUDE.md` names, read by whoever makes or reviews a change there.
   No trailer attests them.
4. **The lints are listed in `tools/README.md`,** which is where a held
   rule's lint is found.
5. **`check` runs on what was pushed.** On a push to `main` the check
   gives `impact.py check` the pushed range, so a pair left unmet on
   `main` fails there as it does on a pull request.

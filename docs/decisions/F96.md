# F96 Faraday Desktop's dice rollers (2026-10-22)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** keys

**Why.** Rolling five dice twice for each of 26 characters per share is
long, and Faraday Desktop is for trying Faraday out: it is testnet only
for real seeds. The owner asked for dice on screen there. The rule of
`docs/CODEX32.md` §5 is that Faraday's own randomness never makes a
fresh seed in these flows; this is its one exception. The plan is
`docs/CODEX32.md` §5.5 and §5.6.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-22).

- On **Faraday Desktop only**: Make codex32 shares gains five dice, the
  de-biasing worksheet's five tracks, rolling one pass, one character
  (the default), one share or to the end per click; New key's dice for
  BIP-39 words and Create a vault's Generate with dice gain one die,
  rolling 1 (the default), 5, 10, to the end or a number typed per
  click.
- Their values are this computer's randomness (`Faraday::fresh`), not
  dice: the row above the dice reads "Rolled by this computer", each
  value it made is marked so on the sheet and in its working, and the
  result's entropy line reads "simulated dice" ("simulated dice and
  your dice" where both were used).
- The entry for real dice stays beside the roller, laid out for codex32
  as the de-biasing worksheet's five dice by two rolls, so the person
  uses their own rolls, Faraday's, or both.
- The stick and the Pi have no roller: real dice are typed on the pad
  there.
- Under Reduce motion the dice do not tumble and the values land without
  a pause.

# F138 The floating action takes Material's primary colour style (2026-10-27)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** review

**Why.** Home's **Scan**, Faraday's one floating action, was filled in
primary-container, Material's default FAB colour. In Cobalt that is the
seed's own #0047AB in Light and Dark, a brighter blue than the filled
buttons and the mark, which are primary (#00327D in Cobalt Light). The
website draws its own call to action, Take the tour, in primary, beside
the shield.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-27). The floating action takes Material
3's *primary* FAB colour style: its container primary, its icon and
word on-primary, its state layer on-primary (`docs/THEMES.md` §7). Its
shape, size, elevation and place are unchanged. It matches the mark
and the filled buttons in every theme, mode and contrast. Cobalt stays
the default theme.

Amends F113's component table (`docs/THEMES.md` §7).

# F246 Off mainnet, the mode pill stands at the end of the top bar's first line, and a long name steps down, then wraps (2026-11-11)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Replaces F223. The title step-down of F139 and the "Back to" line of F146 are otherwise as they were.*

**Why.** F223 put the pill on the bar's second line so the name kept the
first, and the owner found the pill odd down there, on the Pi's 7-inch panel
and elsewhere: a status chip belongs with the name, on the top line (owner,
2026-11-11). Beside the name it cut long ones ("Convert a seed", "Add a
key", the codex32 share names), so the owner chose that the name steps down
a size and, if it still does not fit, wraps; nothing is cut.

**Decision.**

1. **Off mainnet the pill stands at the end of the bar's first line**,
   vertically centred on the page's name's line, its right edge a gap left of
   the tools, on every size that shows it in the bar. Mainnet has no pill and
   is unchanged.
2. **The name has the first line up to the pill**: title large where it fits,
   else title medium, else it wraps at a word onto the second line in title
   medium; where two lines of title medium do not fit the bar, in the next
   smaller title role that does, never growing the bar. Only where one word
   does not fit a line is the name shortened at a word with an ellipsis, as
   before. Cheap: a few widths measured.
3. **Where the name wraps it takes both lines and "Back to" is left off**; the
   chevron stays and its tooltip still says where it goes. Where the name
   fits one line, "Back to" is on the second line and has the width up to the
   tools.
4. **The pill is drawn whole, a full touch target inside the bar, and still
   opens the mode sheet.**

**Against Material.** Material's small top app bar has one title line and
trailing icons. A title that wraps onto a second line is a difference: a long
name is shown whole rather than cut, and the pill is one more control, drawn
whole. Nothing Material requires is withheld.

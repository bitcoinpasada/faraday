# F279 Ready plans on the backup board: paper only, paper and a vault, two vaults (2026-11-16)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** backup
**Held by:** review

*Replaces the first item of the decision that removed the vault presets (2026-10-20, `docs/BACKUP-FLOW.md` §17.21 item 1): the board offers ready plans again. The rest of it holds: no Start from card, a new plan opens on The standard way, a SeedQR is added in Customize backup.*

**Why.** With the presets gone, Customize backup had no starting point
but paper only: a person who wanted a vault built it chip by chip.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-16).

- The read-only board offers ready plans as buttons: Paper only (The
  standard way), Paper and a vault, and Two vaults on two sticks (a single
  key only). Pressing one replaces the placement with that plan's; the
  board stays read-only until Customize backup.
- A vault a ready plan adds is a new one, made in the checklist with its
  own passphrase and written at the write step (`docs/BACKUP-FLOW.md`
  §17.21 item 4 stands).
- None is offered where the plan already holds a vault brought in from a
  stick before the keys.
- The spec is `docs/BACKUP-FLOW.md` §17.25.

**Not built yet.** The buttons are not yet drawn on the board: the plans
are placed by `BoardPress::Plan`, and the screen that shows the buttons
takes them from `board/plans.rs`.

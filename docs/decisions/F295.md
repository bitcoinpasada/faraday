# F295 No Cryptography button on the top app bar: the Guide's tab is the way in (2026-11-19)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** screens
**Held by:** tests

*Amends F148 point 5 (2026-11-19): a screen's Cryptography button and its row in the compact bar's overflow menu are gone, and so is DESIGN.md's Cryptography row of the bar. The rest of F148 holds: the manifest, the blocks, the tab.*

**Why.** The bar carried two buttons for one place: the ? and, beside it,
Cryptography. The second took room from the page's name, the way back and
the eye, and sent the ? into the ⋮ on a compact bar, for something the
Guide already shows on its own tab.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-19).

1. **The bar carries the ? only.** No Cryptography button on the top app
   bar and no row for it in the compact bar's ⋮ menu, in Faraday, Faraday
   Demo and the Starter.
2. **The Guide's Cryptography tab is the way in.** The ? opens the Guide;
   where the screen offers an action of the manifest, the Cryptography tab
   beside the Guide tab shows its blocks, in the sheet and in the
   supporting pane, as before.
3. **The Starter's Guide carries its blocks.** The Starter has no such
   tab: its Guide destination has a **Cryptography** list item, before its
   guide pages, which opens a sheet of the blocks of everything the Starter
   computes, with **Close** at its foot.

Material pages: `https://m3.material.io/components/top-app-bar/guidelines`,
`https://m3.material.io/components/lists/guidelines`.

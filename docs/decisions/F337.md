# F337 A step's fade through from or to a card that shows a secret runs; away from one it reads one kept, wiped copy of the last frame, emptied when its fade out ends (2026-11-27)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests (runtime), tests (root)

*Amends F318 (2026-11-27): point 7's first sentence ("a frame that showed a secret keeps no image
to fade from") gives way to point 2 below for a step's fade through away from a secret card; the
rest of F318 holds. Amends F296 (2026-11-27): point 2's drawing of such a step live from state
during its fade gives way to point 2 below; the rest of F296 holds.*

**Why.** On `main` a step flow moving on from a card that shows a secret (a seed, a key) faded
from the old card for about 48 ms, drawn live from state, and that waited for the owner's approval
(F318 point 7; `docs/FLOW-CONTAINER.md` §3). The specification of `next` drew such a change at
once. The owner was asked (the specification's review, 2026-11-27) to approve the 48 ms fade from
a secret card or keep the change instant, approved it on the board ("Approve the fade") and in chat
("allow the fade"), 2026-11-27. It is a security trade-off: the secret stays on screen about 48 ms
after the press, and a copy of it is kept for that time.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-27; `docs/spec/04-presentation.md` §8.3, rule V19, holds the detail).

1. **What the person sees is what was approved.** A step's fade through away from a card that shows
   a secret runs, its fade out lasting about 48 ms of `STEP_MS` (300 ms); the Motion speed setting
   does not lengthen it.
2. **How on `next`** (the owner chose this way over `main`'s, on the board, 2026-11-27: "Keep the
   picture way"). The step's state, secret included, is dropped at the press (03 A29's `hide`),
   as before; the fade out reads one kept copy of the last frame, in a wiping buffer (02 P13), the
   only kept copy of a frame that showed a secret. It is emptied when the fade out ends or is cut
   short (another change, a lock, a hide, the page left). Turned down: `main`'s live drawing from
   state, which would keep the secret in the step's state past the press.
3. **Towards a secret card too** (owner, in chat, 2026-11-27: "I approve the other case"): a
   step's fade through towards a card that shows a secret runs; the incoming card is drawn live
   each frame, as any incoming card, and no copy of it is kept.
4. **Not covered, so still at once:** a page turn from or to a card that shows a secret, the frost
   under a sheet over one, and a moved frame's reuse of one.

# F136 No way past a check without a legitimate reason (2026-10-27)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Amended by F141
**Area:** security
**Held by:** standards.no-way-past

*Amended by F141 (2026-10-27): an input of the person's key in a miniscript or a taproot tree that no registered wallet gives is left unsigned with a caution on Review, not a stop; the rest of the transaction is signed.*

**Why.** A check that keeps a person's keys or money safe is only as
strong as the way around it. An outside review found a check on a
multisig wallet's change that a person could click past with no real
case for doing so. The owner then asked for every way past a check in
Faraday and the Starter to be reviewed against one rule, and for the rule
to be written where every change is held to it (`docs/STANDARDS.md` §0,
`CLAUDE.md`).

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-27).

1. **The rule.** A check that keeps a person's keys or money safe has no
   way past it unless a decision names a real case that needs one. Where
   one does, the way past is a hold (DESIGN's hold: it fills while held
   and acts only when held to the end), and the hold says what is not
   checked and the case it is for. A transaction that can never be valid,
   or a claim about the person's own key that is false, is refused. A
   note that keeps nothing safe is a note, not a check, and needs no
   hold.
2. **Signing.** `faraday_psbt::sign` has no override. A danger refuses at
   the API unless its kind is holdable and the caller passes that kind by
   name (`accepted`); a kind passed that no hold accepts is refused
   (`Error::NotHoldable`). Only `HighFee` and `AbsurdFeeRate` are
   holdable. A blocked warning refuses whatever is passed.
3. **Review stops** at a transaction nothing signs, with the refusal and
   what to do instead, not only at Sign. No key here taking part
   (`NoParticipatingKey`) is no stop on Review: the spend goes on to
   collect signatures made elsewhere, and signing refuses it.
4. **Refused, which were cautions.** `TimelockNotMet` where no way to
   spend the wallet that the person's keys take part in is open under the
   transaction's sequence and locktime (the chain never accepts what they
   sign; the coordinator sets the sequence or locktime to the wait; while
   such a way is open, the path read from the PSBT is not refused, since
   a cosigner's origin may be left off) and `UnknownDerivation` where a loaded key at a path under its
   account derives another key than the input names (signing refused it
   already) are blocked.
5. **Unverified change is a payment.** An output that claims to be change
   and cannot be verified is drawn under Sends with its address and
   amount, never as Change. Change is only verified change.
6. **Other parties' inputs** (payjoin, coinjoin) keep both refusals: an
   input with no previous transaction (`AmountUnverified`) or no UTXO
   (`MissingUtxo`) refuses whoever it belongs to. An input that names
   none of the loaded keys is never signed; but a coordinator can leave
   off the key origin of the person's own input, so nothing tells another
   party's input from one of the person's own. A hold that signed "your
   inputs" and showed what leaves the wallet from them alone would then
   show less than leaves it over two rounds (round one signs input A with
   B stripped, round two B with A stripped, the same transaction both
   times, the amounts stated truly so the signed-amount memory sees no
   change). That way past waits for a design that closes this.

Refused with no way past, among the dangers: `NONE`, `SINGLE` and a
non-standard sighash; `MissingUtxo`; `NoParticipatingKey`;
`NetworkMismatch`; `InvalidSignature`, `NonceReuse`, a sighash mismatch;
`MusigWalletNotRegistered`. Blocked as before: `ChangeSpoof`,
`UtxoMismatch`, `AmountUnverified`, `UnreadRecords`, `SINGLE` with no
output of its index, a `NONE` signature on the transaction.

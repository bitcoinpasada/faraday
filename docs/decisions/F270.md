# F270 Check a signer takes in Compare two PSBTs: one tile and one Result for two PSBTs or finished transactions (2026-11-15)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active, built
**Area:** signing
**Held by:** review

*Amends F233 (2026-11-15): its point 1's tile is gone; the comparison it
describes is made in Check a signer. Points 2 to 5 hold as written, with
"Compare" read as Check a signer's Result.*

**Why.** Compare two PSBTs and Check a signer took the same two files
from the same places and showed them on the same three cards, and a
person with a PSBT sent to a signer and the one returned, or a
replacement and its original, had to know which tool to open, and
opened both to see the differences and the verdict. One flow answers
both: the differences and the fee bump need only the two files, and the
signer's check runs where both are PSBTs.

**Decision** (`docs/SIGNER-CHECK.md` §11).

1. **One tile.** Compare two PSBTs is no longer a tile in Tools'
   Transactions group, nor an entry in a file's ⋮ in Files. Check a
   signer is the one tile and the one entry: its two cards, Sent and
   Returned, each take a PSBT or a finished transaction, and one Result
   shows the fee bump where F233 point 2 applies (Sent as the original,
   Returned as the replacement), the signer's verdict, the facts, the
   findings and the differences. Its standards are BIP-174, BIP-373 and
   BIP-125.
2. **A finished transaction is never signer-checked.** Where a card
   holds a finished transaction, the Result says "The signer check needs
   two PSBTs" and shows the fee bump and the differences only. Nothing
   the signer's check refused before reaches a verdict now (F84 holds as
   written).
3. **The comparison is unchanged.** Inputs matched by outpoint, outputs
   by script, position never a difference, and no tone on a difference
   (F233 point 1's rules, `docs/COMPARE.md` §2.4); its lines name the
   two files sent and returned in place of first and second.
4. **Sign a transaction's review** keeps its fee bump section and its
   **Compare** button, which opens Check a signer on the pair.

**Where:** `docs/SIGNER-CHECK.md` §11; `docs/COMPARE.md`;
`docs/DESIGN.md` §5, `SignerCheck`.

# F103 A choice that decides a step moves on by itself (2026-10-23)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

**Why.** Many steps asked for a choice and then for **Continue**: Make a
new wallet's **Use vaults** took Yes or No and then Continue, and a Keys
slot's **New key** had a second button, **New key for Key 1**, that did
the same. The owner: always simplify the choices on screen and move the
person on with the fewest presses; they can always go back and change a
choice (F104).

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-23).

- A single choice that decides a step moves the flow on by itself: the
  step closes as done with that choice and the next one opens.
- Pressing the option already chosen moves on too. That is how a
  default is taken.
- No button only repeats the choice just made.
- **Continue** stays where a step holds more than one value (steppers,
  ticks, typing) and where the press is itself the act. Deliberate acts
  stay presses: writing a stick, making a key, wiping, a spend's
  **Sign with**, making a wallet, as DESIGN §1 principle 7, DESIGN's
  Cancel rules (§4.13) and F51 hold them.
- A choice refused (a dial a key cannot turn, an answer a vault already
  open settles) stays on its step with its line.

The owner's answers to the review (2026-10-23):

1. Yes: a single choice moves on, and pressing the option already chosen
   also moves on.
2. Yes: Make a new wallet's Kind and New key's Start open closed on their
   default, like every other flow's card with a default.
3. Yes: **Use vaults** and **How many** are one card: No moves on, and
   Yes shows the count, with one Continue under it.
4. Yes: Keys' Continue reads **Make the wallet**, and Check's reads
   **It matches**, as Restore's do.
5. No: New key still ends on **Done**.
6. Yes: the small panel's board list sheet says **Close**.
7. Yes: the Spend tab's **Starting from the stick** card and the opened
   wallet's card merge into the card after each: Start opens What do
   you have?, headed by what Starting from the stick said, and a wallet
   opening moves on to Check the money, which shows its address.
8. No: the Spend tab's signers stay a deliberate press.

Every step the review marked "keep" stays as it is.

# F329 The Starter's Appearance and About are rows of Home's Settings, not of the menu (2026-11-24)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** Starter
**Held by:** tests

*Amends F91 (2026-10-23): the ⋮ menu holds Uninstall and Remove the boot entry;
About is not in it.
Amends F113 (2026-10-24): the Starter's Appearance is a page opened from
Home's Settings, not from the ⋮ menu. Every other line of both holds.*

**Why.** Home became two panes, and the room it gained holds a Settings
group; two entries of the menu, Appearance and About, are settings a person
looks for, and the menu hid them behind a press.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-23; `docs/STARTER.md` §3.1d). Home's last group
is Settings, with a row for **Appearance** and one for **About**, each opening
its own page. They are reached from Home only: not from the ⋮ menu, Make or
Tools. The ⋮ menu keeps Uninstall and Remove the boot entry and is not drawn
while it has neither.

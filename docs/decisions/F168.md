# F168 The Nonce check page carries the rail's two touch-target exceptions every rail page carries (2026-10-29)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** design.touch-targets

**Why.** `tools/touch-targets.counts` (F152) only falls, and a screen
new to it is a new line. The Nonce check page (`Screen::NonceCheck`,
`docs/FLOWS.md`, Nonce check) draws nothing under 48 dp of its own: the
two targets under the floor at 1280 × 800 are the rail's key chip
(`ExploreKey`) and the top app bar's way back (`Nav`), which every page
beside the rail carries and which the counts already hold for each of
them.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-29). The counts gain
`desktop_1280x800 NonceCheck ExploreKey 1` and
`desktop_1280x800 NonceCheck Nav 1`: the new screen inherits the
rail's existing exceptions. Nothing else is raised. A fix of the rail's
key chip and way back at their source is planned, and lowers these
lines with every other page's.

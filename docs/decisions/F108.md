# F108 Faraday's tokens, colour roles and components follow Material 3, shape included (2026-10-23)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design

**Why.** The standing rule (CLAUDE.md, DESIGN) has every new or changed
screen, component or token follow Material 3, shape among them, while
F91 said "No Material colours, shapes or typefaces". A review of Faraday
and the Starter against Material 3 (2026-10-23) found control edges at
about 1.5:1, buttons drawn 30 to 46 dp tall with the press only on what
is drawn, hover and press that darkened in one theme and lightened in
another, no keyboard focus outside two places, a chosen option drawn as
the screen's one leading button, a disabled primary drawn as a faint
outline, fifteen corner radii, and text as small as 8 dp.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-23, answering the review's questions).
Amends F91: its "No Material colours, shapes or typefaces" becomes "No
Material colours or typefaces"; shape follows Material 3.

- **Shape.** Material's corner scale is named in
  `core/faraday-draw/src/tokens.rs`: none 0, extra-small 4, small 8,
  medium 12, large 16, extra-large 28, full. Each component takes its
  own: buttons and segmented buttons full, cards medium, chips small,
  menus, fields and the toast extra-small, the floating action large,
  dialogs and sheets extra-large.
- **Touch targets.** A press is taken on at least 48 × 48 dp on every
  layout, the target larger than the drawn control where needed, centred
  on it. Two targets that would overlap share the gap between their
  controls, so a target never covers another control. The backup
  board's chips keep their 28 and 20 dp look and take 48 dp targets
  where they do not overlap.
- **Colour roles.** Material's roles that Faraday's names lacked are
  added to every theme, each theme choosing its own value: an outline
  for control edges at 3:1 or more on the page and the card (cards and
  rules keep the fainter edge), a selected colour and the text on it
  (a chosen option, a tonal button), the inverse colours (the toast),
  the accent as it is read (text, icons, the focus ring), and the scrim.
  The values stay each theme's own.
- **Bitcoin Orange Light** draws its text uses of the accent darker, at
  4.5:1 on the page; its fills keep the orange at full strength.
- **States.** Hover, focus and press are Material's state layers: the
  control's content colour over it at 8, 12 and 16 %.
- **Focus.** The keyboard reaches every control: Tab and Shift+Tab move
  through them in the order drawn, Enter or Space presses the one it is
  on, a press of the pointer takes it off; it is ringed 3 dp thick,
  2 dp outside. Each screen's Tab order is set later, screen by screen.
- **Buttons.** Material's filled, tonal, outlined and text buttons;
  text buttons have one look, the label in the accent. A chosen option
  is a segmented button, or where the options do not fit a row, buttons
  with the chosen one in the selected colour and ticked, never the
  screen's filled button. A button not available now keeps its kind.
- **Icons** are 24 dp where they stand alone, 20 in a dense place and
  18 in a button or chip.
- **Text** is never drawn under 11 dp, Material's label-small.
- **Motion.** Material's durations (short 50 to 200, medium 250 to 400,
  long 450 to 600, extra-long 700 to 1000 ms) and easings (standard,
  standard decelerate and accelerate, emphasized decelerate and
  accelerate) are named. A step card opens in 300 ms on emphasized
  decelerate; a switch slides in 150 on standard; a change of theme
  fades in 350 on standard; the toast comes in in 200 on standard
  decelerate and leaves on standard accelerate. A change of screen
  stays a linear 200 ms cross-fade (MOTION §3.5).
- **The toast** is Material's snackbar: in the inverse colours, 48 dp
  tall, shown 4 s.
- **Sheets** keep their frost, under Material's scrim at 32 %.
- **High contrast.** The high contrast themes keep their 7:1 and 3:1
  under everything see-through: no shadows and no fades at a scrolled
  edge; a theme colour asked for see-through is drawn at full strength,
  or, as a tint under text, as its edge, the page left under the text.
  State layers, a control not available now and what fades stay
  see-through, chosen so text on them keeps 7:1.
- **Dialogs** are one component (`faraday_ui::sheet::dialog`): 280 to
  560 dp wide, 24 dp inside, extra-large corners, the actions at its end
  as wide as their labels, sharing the width on the small panel. The
  Starter's and codex32's own dialogs move onto it.
- **Progress.** Long work with no count shows an indeterminate
  progress indicator, still under reduce motion.
- Fields are 56 dp, the desktop's density decided in LAYOUT L5. The
  type scale and the size sets (button, field and row heights) are built
  with LAYOUT L5 and TEXT-SIZE T2, by file owner; codex32's worksheet
  boxes take a press on the nearest box, with ‹ › to step; codex32's
  panels and worksheets become 48 dp secondary tabs.
- Material's colour values, its typeface and its icon set stay
  deliberate differences (F91, F92, DESIGN §3).

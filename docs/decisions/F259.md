# F259 Cryptographic actions carry no fingerprint; a release is held by the cross-check's stamp alone (2026-11-14)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** repository
**Held by:** review

*Amends F197 (point 2: a release no longer runs a review of each changed action, and `just lint-release` is the cross-check's stamp alone), F148 (point 4, the fingerprints, is withdrawn) and F239 (point 4 names the review step that goes).*

**Why.** Each action in `docs/crypto/actions.toml` carried a fingerprint
of its code, its libraries and its vectors, kept in a lock file of
almost ten thousand lines, and a release refused until every action
whose fingerprint had moved was reviewed and stamped again. The stamp
said that someone ran a recipe, not what they read: it is not a security
control, and the code that wrote and checked it was about eight hundred
lines of `docs/crypto/check.py`. What a release changed in the
cryptography is shown as well by a diff since the last release's tag.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-14).

1. **No fingerprints.** The lock file, each action's `fingerprint` and
   `reviewed` fields, and the recipes and code that wrote, re-stamped and
   checked them are taken out. `docs/crypto/check.py` keeps its truth
   and coverage checks.
2. **A release** passes `just lint-release`, which is the cross-check's
   stamp (`tools/lint-crosscheck.sh`) alone, after a fresh cross-check as
   F197 and F239 say. Its notes name what changed in the cryptography by
   a diff since the last release's tag over `core`, `faraday/faraday-keys`
   and `tools/crosscheck`.
3. **The counts stay.** `tools/lint-crypto.counts` is not a copy of the
   manifest's list lengths: each of its counts only falls, and raising one
   is the owner's decision (F164 and F237 for the screens that do not
   offer an action, F178 and F180 for the pieces written here and the
   libraries not established). It stays, `check.py` keeps holding it,
   and those decisions are unchanged.
4. **The guide** no longer says an action was reviewed at a fingerprint.

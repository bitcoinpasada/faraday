# F278 The sidebar no longer holds a session strip (2026-11-16)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** review

*Amends F91 (2026-11-16): the drawer line, "wallets, the session strip, the
sticks", loses the session strip. Every other line of F91 holds.*

**Why.** The strip (Bring in, Open, Work, Write out) repeated the
destinations the rail and Home already carry, and on the Pi's Home it took
room from the cards about this computer.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-16; `docs/DESIGN.md` §5, `Home`;
`docs/LAYOUT.md`).

1. **No strip.** The strip is gone from the small panel's Home and the
   rail's Files row. The sidebar holds what is in progress, keys, wallets
   and the sticks.
2. **Unchanged.** The destinations, their names and what each opens.

**Against Material.** Nothing: a navigation drawer holds destinations, and
these are no longer shown twice.

Material pages: `https://m3.material.io/components/navigation-drawer/guidelines`.

# F175 The vaults and the sticks are laid out together, and each stick is drawn as it comes in (2026-10-30)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** backup
**Held by:** review

**Why.** Make a new wallet and Restore asked how many vaults the backup
uses and then wanted one stick per vault, one at a time in vault order.
A person could not put two vaults on one stick, could not say how many
sticks they would bring, saw nothing of which stick was which, and
could not use the boot stick as one of them; a second Faraday stick was
taken for the boot stick and refused.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-30; `docs/STICK-SETUP.md`).

- One card, **Sticks and vaults**, asks how many vaults and how many
  sticks, the boot stick counted where it is one of them, and lays the
  vaults onto the sticks by dragging or the ⋮ menu, as the backup board
  does. Several vaults on one stick, or sticks and vaults in different
  numbers, are warnings, never refusals.
- Each vault is **New** or **Already on a stick**. A stick holding a
  vault already on a stick is read before the keys; any other stick may
  be checked when it is written, after a dialog that says what that
  means.
- Each stick is drawn on screen with its state as it is brought in, in
  any order, and once read with its maker, model, size and name; a press
  shows the rest.
- The boot stick can serve as any one of the sticks. It is never chosen
  by default. The numbering stays: the boot stick is Boot stick, the
  others Stick 2, Stick 3 and so on (F146).
- The guide says why the sticks are laid out and read here, before the
  keys: a stick is not read while a key or a vault is open.

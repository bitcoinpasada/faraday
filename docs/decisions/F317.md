# F317 Menu pages: each section is one flat card holding a list, several to a row on wide windows (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Proposed
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Not built. `docs/WORKSPACE.md`'s Open line says what is left.*

*Differs from Material 3, where a list stands alone in its page (Lists) and a feed is a grid of equal items (F298): here each section is a filled card (Cards) holding one list, and the cards stand two or three to a row, each as tall as its list. Would amend F298's tiles for Tools and Check.*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-21, with screenshots of Check and Tools at 1920 × 1080): a grid of one outlined card per choice reads as many boxes; a list per section reads as one place per group.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21).

1. The section card is part of the menu page container (`docs/MENU-PAGE.md`): a page declares its sections and the container draws each as a pane, held by `lint-components`. Each section of a menu page (Tools, Check, and every page on the menu page container) is one card holding a Material list: rows with an icon, the title, the line under it and a chevron; hover and press are Material's state layer across the whole row, not a pill; the section's label is the pane's heading inside it.
2. The section cards are flat (filled, surface-container, no shadow). Home's This session pane is the only raised card (F315, F316).
3. On wide windows the cards stand two or three to a row, each as tall as its list, balanced as Settings' cards are; one column on narrow pages. Rows are separated by thin inset dividers, and the phone-size window and the Pi's menu take the cards too (owner, 2026-11-21, if the mock's renders read well).

# F104 Back and Forward move within a flow's steps (2026-10-23)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

**Why.** F77 made Back land on the screen seen before the flow, from
whichever step was open: on Make a new wallet's Keys the mouse's back
button left the flow. The owner: inside a flow, Back goes to the flow's
previous step, not out of it; Home and the other places are reached
through the navigation.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-23). Amends F77: within a flow, Back steps.

- **Back** (the on-screen arrow, the mouse's back button, Escape where
  it is a way back) opens the flow's previous step, with every choice in
  it as it was made.
- **Forward** (the mouse's forward button) opens again the step Back
  left, as it was, while nothing else has been opened since.
- Back from the first step leaves the flow as F77 says: the screen seen
  before, as it was seen.
- A step that is running (a stick being written, a key or a vault being
  made, a search under way) takes no Back.
- After a point that made something the flow cannot take back (a wallet
  made from its seeds, a transaction signed here, a MuSig2 round opened
  here), Back goes back only as far as the step after that point, and
  Back from there leaves the flow. A flow with a way to take it back
  (Check's Path **Change**, F98) keeps it.
- A secret shown on a step is hidden when Back leaves the step; Forward
  opens the step with it hidden.
- **Cancel** still leaves the flow (F77, F51).
- The arrow is named after where it lands: in a flow, the previous
  step's name; on the first step, the screen before.
- Everything else in F77 stands.

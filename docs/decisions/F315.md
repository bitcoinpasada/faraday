# F315 Home's "This session" card has two columns on wide windows and is raised at the Scan button's level (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Differs from Material 3, where an elevated card is level 1 (Cards: https://m3.material.io/components/cards/guidelines); amends F303 and F313 where they differ. F323 (not built) would amend points 1, 2 and 4: groups under small headings, every key and wallet, the PSBT a one-column item, the search in the top bar.*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-21): Home's box should stand apart from the search row and read as one card, with what needs the person apart from the keys here.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21).

1. The box is a card headed "This session" (Title Large, SemiBold) inside at its top left, 24 dp under the search row (`HOME_CARD_GAP`) at every window height (owner, 2026-11-22); the card never moves when a lead comes or goes.
2. From 840 dp of card width (`HOME_CARD_TWO_COLS`) it has two columns split by meaning, with no sub-headings: on the left what needs the person (flows in progress, files to write, wallets that need something, Backups), on the right the keys no wallet uses, at most 5 (`HOME_CARD_KEYS`), then All keys with its count; on a short window key rows drop first, All keys staying, and the card scrolls only when even that does not fit. Under 840 dp it is one list in `docs/HOME.md` §3's order with at most 3 key rows. The card scrolls as one. A row's trailing text ("Make a wallet", the count of All keys) is Body M, as the line under a row.
3. The card is raised at the Scan button's elevation (level 3, `ELEVATION_FAB`) with a neutral fill (surface-container-low), where Material's elevated card is level 1. Orange only on the rows that need something.
4. Nothing about what is going on is drawn outside the card: the lead (`docs/HOME.md` §2, every job of its table) stands inside it as its featured first item, right under the heading and across both columns, in the secondary container (the warning container for a job that cannot be done yet), with its icon, line, the line under it and its one filled button. The search row stays above the card. A row of the card that only repeats the lead's job (the flow it carries on, the wallet whose PSBT or spend it is) is not drawn while the lead stands for it. On the rail's windows the prompt row above the box moves into the box the same way. The phone-size window and the Pi's menu are unchanged.

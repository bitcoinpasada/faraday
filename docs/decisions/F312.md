# F312 Scan is the navigation's FAB at the foot of the rail and of the sidebar (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

**Why.** The FAB at the rail's top moved Scan away from the sidebar's, which
stands at its foot. Scan stays in one place, the foot, on every window that
has a rail.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21; `docs/LAYOUT.md` §3 and §15, `docs/HOME.md` §1).
It replaces F305.

- Scan is the navigation's FAB at the foot of both the rail and the sidebar,
  fixed there, never scrolling with the destinations. The rail's is Material's small FAB, 40 dp
  (the icon alone, centred in the rail); the sidebar's is the extended FAB (the
  icon and the word "Scan", only as wide as they are). The destinations stand
  under the shield mark, as high as the mark leaves them.
- The foot gap equals the top gap above the shield: the FAB's bottom is as far
  from the rail's or sidebar's foot as the shield's top is from its top, and
  the sidebar's FAB has its left edge on the shield's left edge.
- On a rail too short for every destination the FAB keeps its place and the
  space under the shield gives way, so no destination meets the FAB.
- This differs from Material 3 Navigation rail (the FAB at the rail's top) and
  from its expanded rail: the owner's reason is that Scan stays in one place at
  the foot on every window.
- No page draws a Scan button of its own on these windows: the floating Scan of
  Home, Check, Wallets, Files, Vaults and Tools is gone from them, and they keep
  no room at their foot for it. The Pi's menu and the phone-size window keep
  Scan floating at the bottom right, on the pages that have it today.
- A tool's own Scan in its card, where it has nothing to read, stays: it is the
  tool's.

Follows Material 3 Floating action button.

# F322 Every page is made of panes: what a page may draw (2026-11-22)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** review

*Not built. `docs/WORKSPACE.md`'s Open line says what is left.*

*Checked against Material 3's Canonical layouts (https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/canonical-layouts/supporting-pane). The rules for groups, blocks and rows are Faraday's own.*

**Why.** The owner: every page is made of panes, so nothing is added piecemeal and no card stands outside one.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-22).

1. A page draws only chrome, up to two panes (side by side or one above the other) plus the guide as a third, sheets, and the toast and the tooltip, the only passing overlays.
2. A pane holds groups under small headings. A group holds rows, then at most one named block: field, chart, QR, scanner frame, progress, chips, pad, worksheet, or canvas, the one block that fills a pane (the backup board's Places, codex32's dice and tree).
3. A row carries one trailing button or one trailing control.
4. A pane has no buttons in its head (back is the top bar's) and no action bar at its foot: the action is a row, or ends a small flow. A wallet's own actions are a "What you can do" group.
5. Dialogs, menus, Locked (over the empty page) and Your choices are sheets. Self-test failed is the one full-window state.
6. The chrome is listed in `docs/WORKSPACE.md` §14, "What a page may draw". The network chip shows only off Mainnet and is pressable; one power button in the top bar opens a sheet with Lock and Power off; the Stick chip goes.
7. `lint-components` and the self-test hold it when it is built, as they hold the menu page and flow containers.

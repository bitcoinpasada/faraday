# F301 A vault whose passphrase was just set ends locked and is unlocked once with it (2026-11-20)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** vaults
**Held by:** tests

*Reverses the rule in `docs/FLOWS.md` that making a vault opens it at once with the passphrase typed twice and that nothing is typed a third time, for Make a new vault and for a new vault made for a copy waiting. It reverses the same for Change passphrase on an open vault, which wrote the new passphrase at the next seal and never tried it.*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-20): a passphrase typed twice proves only that the two matches, not that the vault written with it opens. Opening the file again with it is the proof, and a vault that opened at once with the text still in the form proved nothing the person saw.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-20).

1. When a vault's passphrase is set, by Create a vault or by Change passphrase, the vault ends locked and the person unlocks it once with that passphrase. It is required and cannot be skipped. The passphrase is still typed twice when it is set.
2. The unlock is the vault's ordinary Unlock: with a stick in, the Pull sheet asks first, and a wrong passphrase reads as at any unlock. A flow that opened Create a vault ("Then: {flow}") returns to its step once that unlock succeeds.
3. Change passphrase on an open vault writes the vault at once, closing it, and goes to its Unlock. Where the vault cannot be written yet (no randomness, an item waiting for its target), the new passphrase waits for the next seal as before.
4. Nothing of the old or the new passphrase stays in the app's state after the vault is closed: the new one is dropped with the open vault, and the unlock's field is wiped as at any unlock.
5. A new vault made inside a backup flow is not covered here.

# F308 The guide shows where the reader is: its scrollbar is always shown, its page's title is pinned under the tabs and the "Guide" heading is gone (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Replaces the guide sheet's "Guide" heading and the pane's "Guide" headline, the page title that scrolled away with the text, and the scrollbar that fades in only while the guide scrolls (DESIGN §4.12, Explainer).*

**Why.** The guide often opens from a flow partway down a page. The page's title was scrolled away and the scrollbar was hidden, so nothing showed the reader that the page goes on above. The heading "Guide" only repeated what the tabs and the button that opened it say, and took height.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21).

1. The guide's sheet and its supporting pane show their scrollbar always, at full strength, at any scroll position and on a small panel too, while the page is longer than its view. This differs from the overlay scrollbar of every other page and sheet, which fades in while it scrolls and out after.
2. The current guide page's title (Title L) is pinned under the tabs (or under the sheet's top edge where there are no tabs): it stays where it is wherever the page is scrolled, and the text scrolls under it. The page does not draw its title a second time at its top. The Contents list and the Cryptography tab have no pinned title; with the Pi's keyboard up over the contents' search field the title gives way, as the tabs do.
3. The heading "Guide" at the sheet's top left and the pane's headline "Guide" are removed. The pane's header row keeps the pin and the close button.

**Cost accepted.** The pinned title takes one to two lines of the view, most on a narrow window.

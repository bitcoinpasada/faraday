# F247 The in-app guide replaces Learn (2026-11-11)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** review

*Amends F1, F2, F11, F30, F68, F81, F91, F93, F112, F148, F159, F166, F174, F197 and F232, each where it names Learn or a Learn page (the table below).*

**Why.** Learn spends many words explaining concepts the app does not
need to explain, ordered by concept, so the ? opens a page about a
subject rather than about the choice in front of the person. The
website's user guide is a second account of the same screens, in
walkthroughs that drift from the app (owner, 2026-11-11).

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-11; `docs/GUIDE-IN-APP.md`).

1. **One guide, by place.** Learn is replaced by the guide: one page per
   place (a screen, or a flow where a screen holds several), one section
   per part (a flow's step, a tab, a category), each section keyed
   `<place>.<part>`. Nothing is ordered by concept.
2. **Opened where the person is.** The guide button opens the guide at
   the open step's section (card 1 or card 5 of a flow opens that card's
   section), falling back to the place's lead section, then to the
   contents; it never opens empty.
3. **What a section holds.** The choices the place offers, each under
   its name as the app shows it, with what choosing it does, makes,
   needs and rules out; why a step asks for something in one sentence
   where the person must act on it; a concept only as far as a choice
   needs it. At most 150 words a section and 40 a choice. Plain
   statement of fact.
4. **The button is Guide.** The ? keeps its place, its icon (`help`)
   and its rules on every size; its tooltip and menu row read Guide.
   The rail's and Home's Learn entry becomes Guide and opens the same.
   The guide's screen is the Learn sheet and, on extra-large windows,
   the supporting pane, as drawn today, showing the place's page at its
   section; tabs only where the place offers a cryptographic action,
   Guide and Cryptography; a Contents list reaches every other page.
5. **Learn's pages go.** The concept pages (now removed) and Faraday's own Learn pages
   are taken out; what a choice needs from them moves into its section.
   No concept pages are kept. The Cryptography blocks and constructions
   stay, on the Cryptography tab. The guides to starting a computer from
   the stick stay as they are.
6. **One guide, in the app** (the owner's choice, 2026-11-11). The
   pages are Markdown in `docs/guide/`, read into the app as written.
   The website does not show them: its guide page says the guide is in
   the app and keeps the guides to starting a computer from the stick.
   The walkthroughs, the screen pages, their pictures and
   the walkthroughs' index move to `docs/history/`.
7. **The strings rule** reads: every user-facing string is in
   `faraday/faraday-strings/src/en.rs` except the guide and the
   self-test's check names; what a place's choices mean is its guide
   section, what Faraday is and its notices are About.

# F83 Faraday Demo is inside the Starter, and not published on its own (2026-10-23)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** release

**Why.** Faraday Demo on its own gives a person no way to make a
stick, and the Starter does. A person who downloads the Starter has
everything; a person who downloads only the desktop app has to come
back for the Starter. Two downloads for the desktop app is one too
many.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-22; built as batch DS, `docs/STARTER.md` §5.3).

- Each Starter's payload carries Faraday Demo for its own system,
  Linux and Windows, built `release=1` as F61's files are. Staging puts
  it in, and the payload's manifest stops refusing it.
- The Starter offers it: a Home row, **Open Faraday Demo**, runs it,
  and **Install** puts it beside the Starter with its own menu entry,
  "Faraday Demo", with no check box. Uninstall removes it. This is
  the design F30 removed (batch C1, D20), without its check box.
- Releases stop publishing `faraday-desktop-X.Y.Z-testnet-only-linux-x86_64`
  and `faraday-desktop-X.Y.Z-testnet-only-windows-x86_64.exe`.
- The app itself is unchanged: it starts in Practice, has no Mainnet, and
  carries the seed line where a seed is typed (F81).
- The macOS Starter, when built, carries none: the macOS desktop app is
  out of the plan (F30, `docs/MAC.md` §5) until the owner puts it back.
- Amends F30 (the Starter part: payload, Install, Try) and F61 (the
  standalone files, and "Staging refuses a desktop app in a payload").

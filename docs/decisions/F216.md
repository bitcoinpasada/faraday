# F216 On the Pi, "card or stick" where the app says "stick" (2026-11-05)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Amended by F235
**Area:** stick
**Held by:** strings.medium

**Why.** A Raspberry Pi reads its own SD card and USB sticks, both mounted and
listed together in Files (`faraday/shells/stick/src/files.rs`; the Pi image
has USB storage). Saying "SD card" for every medium called a USB stick an SD
card and never said a stick works. The Pi's words are now short and cover
both.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-05). Replaces F4. Faraday's core keeps
`Medium { Stick, SdCard }` (`faraday-core/src/medium.rs`), told to it by the
shell at start, and the shell picks it as before: the Pi build (armv7), or an
image that recorded the exchange partition on `/dev/mmcblk0`
(`/etc/faraday/exchange`); the snapshot shell takes `--sd-card`. The PC, the
stick image and Faraday Demo stay "stick". On the Pi every string naming the
medium reads "card or stick": the noun ("card or stick"), the plural ("cards
or sticks"), the article form ("a card or stick"), the sentence-start forms
("Card or stick", "A card or stick"), the visit ("Card or stick" in a menu or on Home, "Visit" in the top bar,
which has little room; F235: the top bar's "Visit" and Home's button no longer apply), the
Sticks and vaults card ("Cards, sticks and vaults"), the zone label and the
pill word ("Card or stick 2"). Where the kind matters the full name is "SD
card or USB stick". The glyph stays `Icon::SdCard`. No string builds "a
{noun}" by hand.

What stays "SD card": the medium the Pi started from. A Pi starts only from
its SD card, so "card or stick" there would be false: the boot medium's name
("Boot SD card"), "Use the boot SD card", the pill "Boot card", the boot
import's sentences about it being pulled or put back, and every sentence
about starting the machine from it (`Medium::started_from`: the Start card's
title and text, "Running from the SD card", the Start from steps in the
family guide). Strings the Starter uses about writing a Pi's card are the
Starter's own and are unchanged.

# F334 A flow in progress is a number on its sidebar tab, with no row under it (2026-11-24)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Amends F41 point 1 for the wide sidebar.*

**Why.** The session pane and the mark on the tab stand for a flow; a row under the tab said it a third time.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-23 and 2026-11-24; `docs/WORKSPACE.md` §16, `docs/FLOWS.md`, Flows in progress).

1. On the wide sidebar no row under a tab names a flow. The tab carries the number of flows in progress there, where its other badge stands, under that badge where it has one; a flow's tab is lit while its flow is on screen. Back up's is under Wallets.
2. The rail and the phone's bar keep their chips until the session pane stands at those sizes.

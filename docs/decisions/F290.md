# F290 One Files page whether a stick is in or not (2026-11-19)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** stick
**Held by:** tests

*Amends F235 (2026-11-19): the stick is no longer a pane that comes and goes on Files, there is no "Then:" chip on Files, and pulling a stick keeps the person on Files unless a flow or a vault sealed for the visit is waiting. The rest of F235 holds: the stick is no destination, Files' badge, Home's lead tile, and "Lock to use {stick}" while anything is held.*

**Why.** Files drew two different pages: its own lists when no stick was
in, and the stick pane when one was, with a row of stick buttons above the
cards, a "Then: Home" chip, a "Remove the stick when you are done" banner
and a Scan a QR code button the Scan button already gives. A stick coming
or going changed the whole page under the person, and the same file moved
between a card on one page and a row on the other.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-19).

1. **One page.** Files is the same page with or without a stick: under the
   top app bar ("Files", the stick status chip, the mode chip and icons),
   two cards from under the bar to the foot of the page. From expanded
   windows up they stand side by side; below that they are Material's
   primary tabs **In Faraday · On {stick}**, both always present.
2. **In Faraday** holds everything Faraday holds: the Inbox summary, a row
   per open vault that opens it in Vaults, the settings, the Outbox groups,
   the Inbox rows, the last write's receipt, and at its foot Write N files
   while a stick is in, else the line saying files stay in memory until
   power-off. Each row has its main action and a ⋮ menu with the rest;
   Select all sits in the card's heading while a stick is in. The row's ⋮ loads
   the vault's wallets with their seeds.
3. **On the stick** holds the stick: its files, and at its foot Delete
   files, Import N files and Import and load, with Rename stick and the stick chips (one per stick, the first named Boot
   stick, which also choose the stick when more than one is in) in its heading.
   With no stick it is an empty state, "Waiting for {stick}", with no
   buttons; with a stick in while anything is held, "Lock to use {stick}"
   with a Lock button. The stick's error line shows here, and on a small
   screen also above the In Faraday list. A one-line status at the foot of
   the card says what pulling does next, while a stick is in and there is
   something to say: "N keys copied in · remove the stick to load them",
   "Remove the stick to unlock {vault}" or "Written and checked".
4. **Pulling the stick** keeps the person on Files; only On the stick goes
   back to its empty state. A stick pulled partway through a flow still
   returns to that flow, and one pulled after a vault was sealed for the
   visit still goes to Unlock, with no chip announcing either.
5. **Gone from Files:** the "Then:" chip, the "Remove the stick when you
   are done" banner, the Scan a QR code button, and the row of stick
   buttons and per-stick filter chips above the cards.

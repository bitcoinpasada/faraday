# F310 A flow's card and Your choices stand as one unit, with Guide's pane beside it (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Differs from Material 3, where a supporting pane is not given a neighbour that moves for it.*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-21): Your choices pinned at the window's edge left the card alone in the room beside it, far from its own list on a large monitor, and Guide, which had to be a sheet in a flow so the two panes never stood together, covered the step it explained.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21).

1. On the wide layout a step whose card is a column and Your choices are one unit: the card's column, a 24 dp spacer and the pane, centred in the room beside the navigation. Two lines frame the card, one the spacer before Your choices and one the same spacer left of the column, each from the top to a page margin above the window's foot; they move with the unit.
2. In such a flow the ? opens Guide's supporting pane at the right, instead of the sheet, whenever the unit and the pane fit. The unit moves left only as far as the pane needs, and stays there when the pane closes, until the flow ends; a new flow starts centred. The move rides the frame's cross-fade that a pane coming or going has.
3. Before Guide falls back to the sheet, the card's column narrows from 720 to 560 dp, and then Your choices from 320 to 240 dp, each only as far as the room needs. The pane fits from 1536 dp of window with the 240 dp sidebar; at 1920 dp the unit moves about 140 dp; from 2560 dp it does not move.
4. The full-width steps and the steps whose card stands at the navigation's edge keep their own pane, or none, and Guide stays the sheet there.

Built in `flow/unit.rs`; `docs/FLOW-CONTAINER.md` §4 and `docs/LAYOUT.md` §5, §6 say it.

# F105 The boot guides give written directions, not mock-ups of firmware screens (2026-10-23)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** Starter
**Held by:** review

**Why.** F18 drew a sketch of each firmware screen on the maker pages.
A maker's setup screen differs by model and BIOS version, and many of
the sketches did not look like the screen in front of the person
(owner, 2026-10-23: a Dell's setup screen looks nothing like its
sketch). A picture that is wrong sends a person looking for something
that is not there; words that name the menu and the setting hold on
every version.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-23).

- *Amends:* F18. The firmware sketches are removed: the `::sketch <id>`
  marker, the data, the drawing, the text-mode face and colours only they
  used, and the line "Mock-up: the real screen may look different."
- The maker pages say in words what to look for: the key to press (as
  `docs/STARTER.md` §7.6), the menu or tab, by the name the maker's own
  documents give, with the common variants ("Boot", "Startup", "Boot
  Options", "Security"); the setting's name ("Secure Boot", "USB Boot",
  "Boot Order" or "Boot Sequence", "Legacy" or "CSM", "Fast Boot"); and
  what to choose. Written as "Look for …", as plain statements of fact.
  Where a maker's documents name nothing, the page says what the setting
  is usually called and that names vary. The page for another maker stays
  general.
- Every page shows the same text in every app and on the website.
  Nothing in the pages is Starter-only any more.
- Nothing else changes: the keys, the steps and their order, and what
  `faraday_learn::MAKERS` reads from the pages (§7.6).

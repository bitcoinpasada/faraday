# F140 The mark in golden proportions (2026-10-27)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** none

**Why.** The shield's rim was a second, smaller shield drawn inside the
first, so its width changed around the outline, and the outline's and
the keyhole's proportions were the boot logo's pixel grid's, not chosen.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-27).

**The band is a true inset.** The band is 7 units wide everywhere on the
100 by 135.3 shield: the inner shield is the outer one moved in by 7
(its corners rounded at 4.33), not a shape of its own.

**The outline is golden.** The sides run straight to the height over the
golden ratio (83.62), then curve to the tip as before; the top corners
are rounded at the band times the golden ratio (11.33).

**The keyhole is golden.** Its head is as wide as its foot, the inner
width over the golden ratio squared (86 / 2.618 = 32.85); the slot runs
from the head's centre to the foot the head's width times the golden
ratio. Two things are not golden:

- The keyhole's middle, between the head's top and the foot, stays where
  F137 put it (56.8 units down): placed by the golden section it stood
  too high in the shield, the fault F137 corrected.
- The neck, where the slot meets the head, keeps its proportion to the
  head (9 to 30, now 9.86): the head over the golden ratio squared made
  the neck wide enough that the keyhole read as a bell.

The colours are unchanged. `Ui::mark` draws it in the app and the
Starter's icon (`faraday/shells/starter/assets/icon.png`, and the Windows
icon made from it); the boot logo's grid
(`faraday/image/common/faraday-mark.txt`) is it at 17 by 23 squares,
set by hand (owner, 2026-10-27): the nearest-pixel copy made the edge two
squares wide on the lower slants, so the edge is one square all round with
a one-square stair to the tip, and the keyhole keeps a one-square neck.

**Amends.** F137 (the keyhole's place), F10 (the keyhole shield beside the
wordmark).

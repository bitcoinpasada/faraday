# F316 This session is Faraday's hub: one card of what is loaded, in a few forms, its place on a page a setting (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Proposed
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Not built. `docs/WORKSPACE.md`'s Open line says what is left. Proposed: the owner picks from renders where the Spec lists choices.*

*Differs from Material 3, which has no persistent-context component: the card is Material's card holding a list (Cards, Lists), single and multiple selection rows, and a collapsed summary row. Would amend F303, F313 and F315 as point 5 says.*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-21): what is loaded is shown a different way in every tool (a row of pills of varying widths), and Home's "This session" card is the model for showing it everywhere and reaching it from inside any flow.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21; open points left to the owner's pick from renders).

1. One component draws what is loaded (keys, wallets, files, flows) from one source, in forms: full on Home (F315); compact on a menu page; a picker in a flow's key step (radio rows for one, checkbox rows for several); folded to one tonal line ("Using: Savings") once the pick is made, pressable to change; Files' In Faraday pane. Every key step's pill row is replaced by the picker. When a flow opens beside the card, the card turns to choosing in one transition (owner, 2026-11-21): its heading becomes the flow's question ("Choose a key"), its key rows become selection rows with a tonal container, it takes a primary outline, and the flow's first screen says "Choose a key" with nothing competing; not chips. Which rows the card holds on a menu page, and whether a key the tool cannot take leaves or stays dimmed with its reason, is the owner's pick from renders.
2. Off Home the card keeps Home's family, the heading in Title M and lower than Home's level 3; level 1, level 3 or none is the owner's pick from renders.
3. The card's place on a menu page is a setting: Automatic (the default), Above the page, Left of the page. Automatic decides when a page opens, never while the person is on it: above while the card's rows fit a short strip (`HUB_ABOVE_ROWS`, counted in rows), to the left once it holds more, showing its whole list there. Where Left does not fit the window, Above is used whatever the setting, and Settings shows the value in force.
4. A tool picked from a page the card stands on opens its flow with the card where it was; the key is picked in it, then the card's other rows fade away and it folds, and the flow takes the focus, the same in either place. Wallets start, Wallets, Vaults, Backups and Explore, which list the same things, do not show it (proposed; the owner picks).
5. Amends: F303 point 3 (Home's box becomes one form of the hub card, which also stands off Home); F313 point 1 (the card scrolls within itself on every page it stands on, not on Home alone); F315 point 2 (the cap on key rows holds above a page; beside one the card lists every key).

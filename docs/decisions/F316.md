# F316 This session is Faraday's hub: one pane of what is loaded, in a few forms, its place on a page a setting (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Proposed
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Not built. `docs/WORKSPACE.md`'s Open line says what is left. Proposed: the owner decided every choice on 2026-11-21; nothing is built until the owner approves the mock.*

*Differs from Material 3, which has no persistent-context component: the pane is Material's card holding a list (Cards, Lists), single and multiple selection rows, and the flow's Your choices pane (F319). Would amend F303, F313 and F315 as point 6 says.*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-21): what is loaded is shown a different way in every tool (a row of pills of varying widths), and Home's "This session" pane is the model for showing it everywhere and reaching it from inside any flow.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21, every choice decided).

1. One component draws what is loaded (keys, wallets, files, flows) from one source, in forms: full on Home (F315); compact on a menu page; a picker in a flow's key step (radio rows for one, checkbox rows for several); once the pick is made, the flow's Your choices (F319), the pick its first row ("Key: Savings"), pressable to change; Files' In Faraday pane. Every key step's pill row is replaced by the picker. When a flow opens beside the pane, the pane turns to choosing in one transition (owner, 2026-11-21): its heading becomes the flow's question ("Choose a key"), its key rows become selection rows with a tonal container, it takes a primary outline, and the flow's first screen says "Choose a key" with nothing competing; the owner chose the selection rows over the keys as chips. On a menu page the pane holds, in a mode of its own per page, the user's own data that page's tools take, whatever its kind (keys, wallets, files, flows), leading with what those tools need most: on Check the files Faraday holds first, then wallets, keys with a seed and flows; on Tools the files a tool takes when one waits, else keys, then wallets and flows (`docs/WORKSPACE.md` §2). A key a tool cannot take stays in the picker, dimmed, with its reason.
2. Off Home the pane keeps Home's family, the heading in Title M and lower than Home's level 3; level 1 (the owner's choice over level 3 and none).
3. The pane's place on a menu page is a setting, and never depends on motion: Automatic (the default), Above the page, Left of the page. Automatic decides when a page opens, never while the person is on it: above while the pane's rows, of whatever kinds the page shows, fit a short strip (`HUB_ABOVE_ROWS`, 3 rows), to the left once it holds more, showing its whole list there. Where Left does not fit the window, Above is used whatever the setting, and Settings shows the value in force.
4. A tool picked from a page the pane stands on opens its flow with the pane where it was; the key is picked in it, then the pane's other rows fade away and it becomes the flow's Your choices at the left (F319; with the pane above, the strip glides to the left), the same from every page. Wallets start, Wallets, Vaults, Backups and Explore, which list the same things, do not show it. Files shows it at the left as In Faraday, keeping that heading. On Settings the pane stands at the left with Settings' cards beside it, and also under it on a tall window: the pane takes at most half the page's height (`HUB_CARD_HEIGHT_SHARE`), its rows scrolling inside, and Settings' cards fill the column under it where at least `HUB_UNDER_MIN` (800 dp) is left.
5. **One component, one source** (owner, 2026-11-21). The pane is one component, a container as the flow and menu page containers are: its forms (Home, a menu page above and left, the key picker, Your choices, Files) are modes of it, fed by one source; no page draws its own list of what is loaded. The page with the pane beside or above it is a layout container of its own, which a page or flow declares itself into and which owns the pane's place (point 3), the glide and the use of the motion settings (F318). Each is held by `lint-components` and has its own document, written when it is built (`docs/WORKSPACE.md` §14), and CLAUDE.md's "By area" names them then.
6. Amends: F303 point 3 (Home's box becomes one form of the session pane, which also stands off Home); F313 point 1 (the pane scrolls within itself on every page it stands on, not on Home alone); F315 point 2 (the cap on key rows holds above a page; beside one the pane lists every key).

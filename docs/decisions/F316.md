# F316 This session is Faraday's hub: one pane of what is loaded, in a few forms, its place set by the page (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Not built. `docs/WORKSPACE.md`'s Open line says what is left. The owner decided every choice on 2026-11-21 and approved the mock on 2026-11-22; nothing is built yet.*

*Differs from Material 3, which has no persistent-context component: the pane is Material's card holding a list (Cards, Lists), single and multiple selection rows, and the flow's Your choices pane (F319). Would amend F303, F313 and F315 as point 6 says. F331 (not built) would amend point 2: Home's level and the pane's are one, and in a flow the pair alone is raised, at level 2.*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-21): what is loaded is shown a different way in every tool (a row of pills of varying widths), and Home's "This session" pane is the model for showing it everywhere and reaching it from inside any flow.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21, every choice decided).

1. One component draws what is loaded (keys, wallets, files, flows) from one source, in forms: full on Home (F315); compact on a menu page; a picker in a flow's first step (radio rows for one, checkbox rows for several); once the pick is made, the flow's Your choices (F319), the pick its first row ("Key: Savings"), pressable to change; Files' In Faraday pane. Every key step's pill row is replaced by the picker. When a flow opens beside the pane, the pane turns to choosing in one transition (owner, 2026-11-21): its heading becomes the flow's question ("Choose a key"), its key rows become selection rows with a tonal container, it takes a primary outline, and the flow's first screen says "Choose a key" with nothing competing; the owner chose the selection rows over the keys as chips. On a menu page the pane holds, in a mode of its own per page, the user's own data that page's tools take, whatever its kind (keys, wallets, files, flows), leading with what those tools need most: on Check the files Faraday holds first, then wallets, every key and flows; on Tools a waiting PSBT and the held files, else keys then the held files, then wallets and flows; on Wallets keys, a waiting PSBT, held wallet files and flows, not the wallets (owner, 2026-11-22; `docs/WORKSPACE.md` §2). An item of the right kind that a tool cannot take stays in the picker, dimmed, with its reason; a held file of a kind it never takes is hidden.
2. Off Home the pane keeps Home's family, the heading in Title M and lower than Home's level 3; level 1 (the owner's choice over level 3 and none).
3. **The pane's place is set by the page and the room, with no setting** (owner, 2026-11-22), and never depends on motion or on how much is loaded. On Home it is centred. On a page entered from Home it stands at the left with the page's content in one wide tile at its right, the pair centred, level at top and bottom, never longer than its content, at the place and widths of a flow's pair, so that entering a flow moves nothing sideways. Tools and Check stack their tile under This session where everything fits. A setting to pin the pane at the left does not ship.
4. The key or item is picked in the pane, then the pane's other rows fade away and it becomes the flow's Your choices at the left (F319). Wallets has its own pane and does not stack; Files keeps In Faraday at the left; Vaults has two panes, what its pane holds not decided; Settings has no pane and opens under Home's card where all of it fits; the backup board has no This session (owner, 2026-11-22).
5. **One component, one source** (owner, 2026-11-21). The pane is one component, a container as the flow and menu page containers are: its forms (Home, a menu page, the key picker, Your choices, Files) are modes of it, fed by one source; no page draws its own list of what is loaded. The page with its panes is a layout container of its own (F322), which a page or flow declares itself into and which owns the pane's place (point 3), the glide and the use of the motion settings (F318). Each is held by `lint-components` and has its own document, written when it is built (`docs/WORKSPACE.md` §14), and CLAUDE.md's "By area" names them then.
6. Amends: F303 point 3 (Home's box becomes one form of the session pane, which also stands off Home); F313 point 1 (the pane scrolls within itself on every page it stands on, not on Home alone); F315 as F323 says.

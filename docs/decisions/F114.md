# F114 Wide content takes the room beside the navigation; reading content keeps its column (2026-10-24)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design

On wide windows a page uses the window, on the owner's ask (2026-10-24,
from Make codex32 shares' dice entry at about 2560 dp wide):
`docs/LAYOUT.md` §13 to §19.

- **The page area is the window less the navigation.** The 1600 dp cap
  on the navigation and the page together goes (`MAX_W`); what stands
  at the right (the mode pill, the ?, the eye, the keyboard button,
  Scan, a supporting pane) stands `MARGIN_WIDE` from the window's edge,
  as Material's pane scaffold and top app bar put them.
- **Two widths, by what is drawn.** Reading content (text, fields,
  buttons, card heads, rows) keeps `COLUMN_MAX` and `MEASURE`, as
  Material keeps line length and gives a wider window more panes.
  Wide content (worksheets, the dice tracks and tree, the board, the
  structure chart, the copy grid, tables, the tile grid) takes the work
  width, from the navigation's margin to the supporting pane. This
  departs from one 720 dp column for every step flow (§5): a flow's
  open card that holds wide content runs to the work width.
- **Larger cells, not stretched ones.** Wide content shows more of
  itself at once, or draws its cells larger between token bounds
  (`CODEX32_CELL_FIT_MIN` to `CODEX32_CELL_FIT_MAX` for the checksum
  worksheet, `CODEX32_STRING_BOX_MIN` to `CODEX32_STRING_BOX_MAX` for a
  string's boxes); where it does not fit at the least size it keeps
  today's view, panning both ways.
- **What a person presses stands with what it fills.** The dice pad,
  the dice's size and the rolls form one input panel: beside the sheets
  where the work width holds both, pinned at the window's foot
  otherwise (Material's bottom-anchored input), in the page where the
  panel would take more than 40 % of the window's height; the Pi panel
  as before.
- **1280 × 800 keeps its layout** but for the pad's place: the cap did
  not bind there, and the worksheet does not fit, so it pans as before.
- **The string's row of 24 is Faraday's.** The booklet and
  secretcodex32.com set no row length for writing a string out; the
  groups of four and the box numbers stay the booklet's.

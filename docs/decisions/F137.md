# F137 The keyhole sits lower in the shield (2026-10-26)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design

**Why.** The keyhole's centre stood at 0.389 of the shield's height,
above the shield's optical centre (the centroid of its area, 0.446),
so the mark read top-heavy, most at icon sizes.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-26, from renders of each option).

**The keyhole is lowered 3% of the shield's height.** `Ui::mark`
draws the keyhole's head centred at 0.31 of the shield's height (was
0.28) and the slot's foot at 0.64 (was 0.61); head and foot move
together and their shapes are unchanged. The Starter's menu icon
(`faraday/shells/starter/assets/icon.png`, and the Windows icon made
from it) is drawn again from the new mark. The boot logo's pixel grid
(`faraday/image/common/faraday-mark.txt`) moves its keyhole down one
row, the nearest whole row: its centre goes from 9/23 (0.391) to
10/23 (0.435) of the grid's height, 0.014 past the lowered mark's
0.421, where staying would leave it 0.030 short.
**Amends.** F10 (the keyhole shield beside the wordmark).

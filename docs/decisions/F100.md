# F100 The 7-inch and 10-inch panels run in landscape, with portrait a setting (2026-10-23)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

**Why.** F92 lays Faraday out by window size class, so either
orientation of a panel gets the class its width gives it. Faraday's
screens do not favour one orientation. The backup board, the structure
chart, Explore and codex32's worksheets are wide; list and detail needs
840 dp beside the navigation; and a device on a desk sits steadier, and
like a monitor, in landscape. Typing words and passphrases on the
on-screen keyboard has more room in portrait, where the keyboard does
not take half of a 580 dp height, and so does reading long text.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-23). Amends F92.

- The 7-inch and 10-inch panel builds start in landscape.
- Portrait is supported at both sizes and chosen in Settings, kept on
  the stick; the panels have no sensor to tell which way up they are.
  A rotated screen rotates its touch input with it.
- Portrait gets the class its width gives it: the 7-inch panel (about
  580 × 960 dp) is compact, with the bar at the foot and one pane at a
  time; the 10-inch panel (about 800 × 1280 dp) is medium, with the
  rail.
- Renders are checked at both orientations of each panel size.
- The user guide says landscape suits the backup board and list and
  detail, and portrait suits typing words in.

**Answers** (owner, 2026-10-27; `docs/ORIENTATION.md` §14, each as
recommended):

- A screen of 11 inches or less is laid out at the density its EDID
  gives; `faraday.dpi` is kept for every other screen (amends F101). At
  about 160 dpi the 7-inch panel in portrait is 600 dp wide, medium,
  not compact as above.
- The stick's panels come first: a 7-inch 1024 × 600 panel, landscape
  natively, and a 10-inch 800 × 1280 panel, portrait natively. The Pi's
  panels follow as their own work.
- Portrait is offered on a panel that is landscape natively, on panels
  of 6 to 11 inches; never on a laptop's screen or a monitor.
- The half turn and the opposite quarter turn are not offered; the touch
  map is drawn and tested at all four.
- The boot logo and the kernel console stay as the firmware draws them.
- Faraday Demo's Size menu gains the two portrait rows, and its 7-inch
  and 10-inch rows their real densities.

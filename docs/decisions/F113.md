# F113 Colour follows Material 3's colour system: ten seeded themes, Light and Dark, three contrast levels (2026-10-24)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design

**Why.** F108 took Material 3's colour roles into Faraday's themes but
kept each theme's values its own, tuned by hand: fourteen tables, five of
them borrowed from other projects' palettes, and two separate high
contrast themes. Each new role meant fourteen hand-picked values and a
contrast test to tune them against. Material 3's colour system makes a
whole scheme from one seed colour by a published method, with contrast
levels built in, and Google's material-color-utilities implements it.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-24; the spec is `docs/THEMES.md`).

- Colour follows Material 3's colour system fully, as if Faraday had used
  it from the start: HCT, tonal palettes, roles taken from tones and
  contrast curves, by material-color-utilities' 2021 colour spec.
- A theme is one seed colour and one Material scheme style: tonal spot
  by default, fidelity where the seed must stay true. Ten themes:
  Cobalt (the default, a cobalt blue near #0047AB, in Light at Standard
  contrast), Bitcoin Orange (#F7931A, fidelity), Lamplight (warm amber),
  Mars (rust red), Forest (green), Frost (cool blue-grey, neutral
  style), Dusk (indigo), Lavender (soft violet), Rose (dusty rose) and
  Monochrome (Material's monochrome scheme). Their seeds are in
  THEMES §2. Cobalt is fidelity on #0047AB, so the exact cobalt stays in
  every Cobalt scheme as primary-container.
- Bitcoin Orange takes Material's scheme as it comes: its filled buttons
  are primary (#8C4F00 in Light), and #F7931A is primary-container.
- Each theme is drawn in Light or Dark, at Standard, Medium or High
  contrast (Material's levels 0, 0.5 and 1.0). Settings offers Theme,
  Mode and Contrast as three choices.
- Retired: the borrowed names, Nord, Catppuccin, Gruvbox, Tokyo Night and
  Rosé Pine; Dark and Light as themes (they are the Mode); Bitcoin
  Orange Light (Bitcoin Orange in Light); the two high contrast themes
  (the Contrast choice replaces them). A stored old theme opens in the
  theme, mode and contrast THEMES §13 maps it to.
- The full Material 3 role set, with OK and Warning as Material custom
  colours, each with its four roles. They are harmonised with the seed,
  except where that would put them near the theme's primary: Warning
  stays unharmonised in Lamplight and Bitcoin Orange, and OK in Forest.
  Each component takes the roles Material's component spec gives it.
- The sidebar is surface-container-low, a slight shade apart from the
  page.
- The Starter draws from the same themes and has the same three
  controls: Theme, Mode and Contrast.
- State layers follow Material 3's current guidance: hover 8 %, focus
  10 %, pressed 10 %, dragged 16 %. Material Web's tokens still give
  12 % for focus and pressed; Faraday follows the guidance. Disabled content is on-surface at 38 %, a disabled
  container on-surface at 12 %. Elevation is shown by surface-container
  tones and Material Web's shadows, with no tint overlay.
- The scheme tables are generated by material-color-utilities, vendored
  and pinned, and compiled in; `just lint` fails when they differ from
  what the pinned reference generates. Faraday ships the tables, not the
  HCT code.
- At High contrast, F108's rules for the high contrast themes carry over
  (no shadows, no fades at a scrolled edge, a see-through theme colour
  drawn at full strength or as its edge), except that a disabled control
  and the state layers are no longer held to 7:1.
- A custom seed colour picker is not scheduled.

**What it replaces.**

- F91: "The look stays Faraday's own: `faraday-ui`'s components,
  DESIGN's tokens and themes. No Material colours, shapes or typefaces."
  (already "No Material colours or typefaces" by F108) becomes "No
  Material typefaces"; and the amendment line's "the look stays
  Faraday's own in its colours and typefaces" becomes "in its
  typefaces".
- F108's lead: "Amends F91: its 'No Material colours, shapes or
  typefaces' becomes 'No Material colours or typefaces'" is amended as
  above.
- F108's **Colour roles** bullet ("Material's roles that Faraday's names
  lacked are added to every theme, each theme choosing its own value …
  The values stay each theme's own.") is replaced: every role's value is
  Material's, from the theme's seed.
- F108's **Bitcoin Orange Light** bullet is withdrawn: Material's
  primary is the text-grade tone by construction, and the orange itself
  is primary-container.
- F108's **States** bullet ("the control's content colour over it at 8,
  12 and 16 %") becomes 8, 10, 10 and 16 % (hover, focus, pressed,
  dragged).
- F108's **High contrast** bullet applies at High contrast, on any
  theme, without its last sentence's 7:1 for state layers and a control
  not available now.
- F108's last bullet ("Material's colour values, its typeface and its
  icon set stay deliberate differences") becomes "Material's typeface and
  its icon set stay deliberate differences".

# F320 Every flow that produces something ends with one take-away pane: QRs and ticked files, one Write to stick (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Proposed
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Not built. `docs/WORKSPACE.md` §15 and its Open line say what is left. Proposed: the owner decided every choice on 2026-11-21; nothing is built until the owner approves the mock.*

*Differs from Material 3 in no part: a level 1 card (Elevation, Cards) holding a list with leading checkboxes (Lists) and one filled button (Buttons), its green the custom role `Ok` beside the scheme (Color). The Spec review checked it against those five pages. Meets F296 (the flow container's last step), F306 (the green row) and F316 and F319 (Your choices at the left).*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-21): a flow that makes something (a signed transaction, a changed vault, a backup, an exported wallet) ends in a different way in each: a Finish button, a "Write to the stick", a "Show as QR", a "Save as a file", the file sitting in Files' to write. The person should find what they take away in one place, drawn one way, and see that it is written and checked in the same way in the flow and in Files.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21).

1. **One pane, built once.** Every flow that produces a file or a QR ends with the take-away pane: the flow's last step, drawn by one container (`WORKSPACE.md` §14), not by each flow. It holds what the person takes away: QRs to scan and files of any kind (the signed PSBT, a vault to write back, backups, an exported wallet).
2. **Where and how it looks.** It is the flow's last step, standing where the step's card stands; Your choices stays at the left (F319). Level 1 as the session card off Home (F316 point 2), not level 3 (that is the FAB, the toast and the notice). Emphasis from its place, a plain heading ("Take with you", in faraday-strings) and one filled "Write to stick" button at its foot, the step's one high-emphasis action. On arrival it carries an emphasis that eases away, as the key moment does, at the Speed setting (F318; at once under Off).
3. **Contents.** The QR on a light square with its quiet zone, in Dark as well; then the files as Files' In Faraday rows (leading checkbox, name, kind, size).
4. **Files come ticked.** "Write to stick" writes the ticked ones; the person unticks what they do not want written. Writing is always a press, never automatic.
5. **Written and checked.** After a write is read back and compared, the rows in the pane and the same rows in Files show F306's green (`Role::Ok` at 12% as a state layer, a check icon at the row's end), matching. It is one state, read from the visit's receipt, in both places.
6. **No stick.** The rows stay ticked and read "Waiting for a stick" (Files' wording); the button returns when a stick is in. Meanwhile the files are in Files' to write. No error colours.
7. **No file moves.** A file the pane shows is the one in Files' to write; the pane is a second view of it, never a copy or a move.
8. **A secret is never kept longer than today's last step keeps it** (F296 point 2): `WORKSPACE.md` §15 says how.

**Alternatives the owner passed over** (2026-11-21):

- Pressing a file queues it in to write: a person must know that a press queues, and a file never pressed is lost from sight.
- Written automatically at the end of the flow: a write to a person's stick without a press, which Faraday does nowhere else (the visit asks).

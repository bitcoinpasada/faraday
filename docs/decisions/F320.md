# F320 Every flow that produces something ends with one take-away: QRs and ticked files, one Write to stick (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Not built. `docs/WORKSPACE.md` §15 and its Open line say what is left. The owner decided every choice on 2026-11-21 and approved the mock on 2026-11-22; nothing is built yet.*

*Differs from Material 3 in no part: a level 1 surface (Elevation, Cards) holding a list with leading checkboxes (Lists) and one filled button (Buttons), its green the custom role `Ok` beside the scheme (Color). The Spec review checked it against those five pages. Meets F296 (the flow container's last step), F306 (the green row) and F316 and F319 (Your choices at the left).*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-21): a flow that makes something (a signed transaction, a changed vault, a backup, an exported wallet) ends in a different way in each: a Finish button, a "Write to the stick", a "Show as QR", a "Save as a file", the file sitting in Files' to write. The person should find what they take away in one place, drawn one way, and see that it is written and checked in the same way in the flow and in Files.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21).

1. **One take-away, built once.** Every flow that produces a file or a QR ends with the take-away, drawn by one container (`WORKSPACE.md` §14), not by each flow. It holds what the person takes away: QRs to scan and files of any kind (the signed PSBT, a vault to write back, backups, an exported wallet).
2. **Where it stands: in the session pane (the owner chose A in the mock, 2026-11-22).** The container is the same either way (points 3 to 8): a plain heading ("Take with you", in faraday-strings), the QR and the files, and one filled "Write to stick" button at its foot, the one high-emphasis action; level 1 as the session pane off Home (F316 point 2), not level 3; on arrival an emphasis that eases away, as the key moment does, at the Speed setting (F318; at once under Off). The session pane at the left turns, with the glide, from Your choices into the take-away plus next steps that follow from the result only (never Tools, Files or Home, which the sidebar has); the step's card states what happened and holds no files; after writing or leaving, the pane settles back to its session form, the written files green there and in Files; on the phone-size window and the Pi the take-away is the one pane, as a step card, and no sheet opens by itself (owner, 2026-11-22). This changes F319 (Your choices at the left in every step but the last) and F296 (the last step is the outcome card).
3. **Contents.** The QR on a light square with its quiet zone, in Dark as well; then the files as Files' In Faraday rows (leading checkbox, name, kind, size).
4. **Files come ticked.** "Write to stick" writes the ticked ones; each file is ticked on its own and the person unticks what they do not want written; an unticked file is not written and stays in Files' In Faraday, held. Writing is always a press, never automatic.
5. **Written and checked.** After a write is read back and compared, the rows in the pane and the same rows in Files show F306's green (`Role::Ok` at 12% as a state layer, a check icon at the row's end), matching. It is one state, read from the visit's receipt, in both places.
6. **No stick.** The rows stay ticked and read "Waiting for a stick" (Files' wording); the button returns when a stick is in. Meanwhile the files are in Files' to write. No error colours.
7. **No file moves.** A file the pane shows is the one in Files' to write; the pane is a second view of it, never a copy or a move.
8. **A secret QR** is drawn a little longer than today's last step draws it. The owner said yes to this trade-off (2026-11-22); it is built and tested on its own, and its results are shown to the owner before it reaches main. `WORKSPACE.md` §15 says how; the QR is drawn live in the session pane from the flow's state and released when the pane settles back, at Lock or idle.

**Alternatives the owner passed over** (2026-11-21):

- Pressing a file queues it in to write: a person must know that a press queues, and a file never pressed is lost from sight.
- The take-away as the last step's card, with the session pane showing only the next steps in place of Your choices (B): it keeps what the person takes away in the middle of the page with the step they just did and leaves the pane with little to show.
- Written automatically at the end of the flow: a write to a person's stick without a press, which Faraday does nowhere else (the visit asks).

# F120 Settings' theme chips are filled in their own theme's colours; the chosen one is ringed (2026-10-25)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design

**Why.** Settings' Theme row showed each theme's colour only as a small
swatch inside a Material filter chip, the chosen chip filled in the
scheme's secondary-container (owner, 2026-10-25). The colour a person is
choosing should be what the chip shows.

**Decision.** Each theme chip (`docs/THEMES.md` §12, DESIGN §4.2) is filled
in that theme's own primary-container, in the mode and contrast on
screen, its name and Default mark in that theme's on-primary-container.
A chip not chosen leads with that theme's primary as a dot and has no
edge; the chosen one leads with the Done tick and is ringed in that
theme's primary, 3 dp thick and 2 dp outside the fill, so the page shows
between ring and fill and the ring reads against the page on both
sides. Primary is the ring in Light and Dark: in Light it is the
darker colour; in Dark a ring darker than the fill would vanish into the
dark page, and primary, the lighter, stands 10:1 or more on it. The
state layer is the chip's own on-primary-container; while it shows, the
label and tick go to white or black, whichever that colour leans to,
so a held chip's label keeps 4:1 (6.5:1 at High).

**Differs from Material.** Material's filter chip is filled in
secondary-container when selected and edged in outline when not; these
chips are filled in their own theme's colours whatever is chosen, and
the selection is the ring and the tick. Material lays the state layer in
the content colour and leaves the content as it is; here the content
moves to white or black under it.

**Amends.** THEMES §12 (the Theme row) and §7's table, which gains the
theme chip's own row; DESIGN §4.2's filter chip row.

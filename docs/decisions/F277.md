# F277 The Check screen shows its checks as tiles in the grid Tools uses (2026-11-16)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** review

*Amends F255 (2026-11-16): point 3, the Check screen is a list, becomes
tiles in the grid Tools uses. Every other point of F255 holds.*

**Why.** Check and Tools are two ways in to the same tools, and the person
who moves between them meets a list in one and tiles in the other. A
tile's label and one line fit a check as they fit a tool, and the grid
uses a wide window's room, where a list of rows leaves it empty.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-16; `docs/DESIGN.md` §5, `Check`;
`docs/LAYOUT.md` §17).

1. **Tiles, not rows.** Each check is one tile in the grid Tools draws
   (one function draws a tile and its columns for both screens), in as
   many columns as fit: its label and one line saying what the person
   needs in hand. A check that needs the seed carries a **Seed** tag, as a
   standard is tagged on a Tools tile.
2. **Unchanged.** The groups, their subheaders, the order, the labels, the
   lines and what each tile opens are F255's and DESIGN §5's, as are the
   screen's name and the rest of F255.

**Against Material.** Nothing: the tiles are Material 3 cards in a grid,
as Tools' are.

Material pages: `https://m3.material.io/components/cards/guidelines`.

# F131 Zeroing a stick's free space or its partition takes a hold (2026-10-27)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** stick

**Why.** The stick visit's Delete files sheet zeroed the free space or
the whole files partition on a press of its button, while deleting a
vault file from the same sheet already took a hold (`docs/FLOWS.md`
decision 4). Zeroing cannot be undone either: what it writes over is
not read back by anything.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-27; `docs/FLOWS.md`, DESIGN §1 item 7 and
§4.13). On the Delete files sheet, **The free space** and **The whole
partition** are done by DESIGN §4.13's hold, "Hold to zero the free
space" and "Hold to zero the whole partition", as a vault file chosen
is deleted: held to its end, zeroing starts; released before, nothing
is written. A press of the sheet's button starts nothing. Chosen files
that are not vaults are still deleted by the button.

# F296 One flow container: one step at a time, at one place, with Your choices beside it (2026-11-19)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** review

*Amends F39 (2026-10-16): a flow's steps are no longer stacked as sections
divided by a rule; only the current step shows (one page colour stands).
Amends `docs/FLOWS.md` "Step cards": a finished step no longer stays above
the open one; it is a row in Your choices. Amends F108 as `docs/MOTION.md`
§3.5 applies it to step cards: a card no longer opens by growing, and the
glide that brought the open card into view goes; a change of step fades
through. Amends F114 (`docs/LAYOUT.md` §14) for a flow: the open card's
rules no longer run the work width, and wide content in a flow ends at
the Your choices pane, except the two full-width steps below. F104 (Back
and Forward within a flow) stands and binds everything here.*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-19): in every step flow the finished steps
pile up above the current one ("Opens on ✓ PCs and a Raspberry Pi" and
the rows after it), the current card moves down the page with each step,
and the page scrolls to follow it. Make a new vault's Your choices panel
already shows each answer once, at the right, and takes a press back to
its step; every flow should work that way, drawn by one piece of code
rather than each flow its own.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-19; `docs/FLOW-CONTAINER.md`).

1. **One card, one place.** Only the current step's card shows, always at
   one fixed place in the flow: where the first step's heading stands
   today. The finished steps are not drawn above it.
2. **Fade through.** Moving to another step fades the current card out and
   the next one in at exactly the same place, over 300 ms: nothing
   scrolls, nothing moves up or down. Back is the same. Under Reduce
   motion the card is swapped at once. A card that shows or takes a secret
   fades too, drawn live from the state it still holds, so no frame with a
   secret is kept; its secret state is released when the fade out ends
   (about 105 ms), not at the press. This is a security trade-off that
   reaches main only on the owner's approval after testing.
3. **Your choices at the right in every flow**, as Make a new vault has it:
   each step's answer as it is given. A press on one opens that step (the
   same fade), and the person goes forward again from there.
4. **Narrow windows** (a half-screen laptop window, a small panel, the Pi's
   panel): a **Your choices** button in the top app bar opens the list as
   a sheet.
5. **An earlier answer changed** keeps the later answers that still fit,
   so the person can go straight forward to where they were, and clears
   those that no longer fit.
6. **Two full-width steps.** Make codex32 shares while its dice rolls,
   tracks and tree are on screen, and the backup board: Your choices is
   hidden and the step takes the whole width; the top app bar's Your
   choices button, as on a narrow window, opens the list as a sheet, so
   the way back stays. Every other dice worksheet (a seed's rolls) stays in
   the main column beside Your choices.
7. **Who owns what.** A flow declares its steps, each step's card, how each
   answer reads in Your choices, which later answers still fit after an
   earlier one changes, and which steps are full width. The container
   owns the card's place, the fade, the Your choices pane and sheet,
   going back and forth, and the top app bar's way back. It grows out of
   today's shared column (`flow::Column`, `faraday/faraday-core/src/flow.rs`),
   not beside it.

**The owner's answers (2026-11-19)** to the Spec's open questions:

- A secret's step fades too, drawn live (point 2).
- The fade is 300 ms (Medium 2), checked on the Pi.
- An existing right panel (Sign a transaction's review, Back up's map,
  the backup board's list) becomes Your choices: the rows first, today's
  content under them.
- Guide opens as a sheet inside a flow; Create vault ends the last card
  and the pane keeps the summary; Back up's checklist is the first card
  with its items as rows; a flow of unknown length lists the steps reached
  and those known to come; the bar's button is an icon with the tooltip
  "Your choices" on the small panel and labelled elsewhere; Your choices
  stays at a flow's end, rows before a point that cannot be taken back
  taking no press; a pane on a low window scrolls within itself.

**Checked against Material 3.**

- *Fade through* (Motion, transition patterns:
  https://m3.material.io/styles/motion/transitions/transition-patterns):
  for elements without a strong relationship, the outgoing content fades
  out, then the incoming fades in. Material Components' own fade through
  (`MaterialFadeThrough`, material-components-android) fades out over the
  first 35% of the transition and in over the rest, scales the incoming
  content from 92% to 100%, on the emphasized easing. Faraday keeps the
  split and the easing.
- *Supporting pane* (canonical layouts:
  https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/canonical-layouts/supporting-pane)
  and *side sheets*
  (https://m3.material.io/components/side-sheets/guidelines): secondary
  content beside the primary, at the trailing edge, a pane of its own
  scroll; on compact windows it moves into a sheet.
- *Bottom sheets* (https://m3.material.io/components/bottom-sheets/guidelines)
  and the modal side sheet for the narrow case.
- *Top app bar* (https://m3.material.io/components/top-app-bar/guidelines):
  trailing action buttons at the bar's end.

**Where Faraday differs from Material**, each named:

- **No scale.** The incoming card does not grow from 92%: the owner's
  "nothing moves" holds, and Faraday's fades blend kept frames, which
  cannot scale.
- **A fixed card, not a scrolling list of steps.** Material's steppers and
  lists keep earlier steps on the page; here the answers live in the pane.
- **The pane's breakpoint.** Material shows a supporting pane from
  expanded windows (840 dp) up; Faraday shows Your choices beside the
  card only on the wide layout (the expanded rail), every one-column
  layout taking the sheet, since a 720 dp column and the pane do not fit
  beside the rail below it.
- **The pane's width and look.** Make a new vault's: 31% of the page area,
  240 to 320 dp, on the surface with a divider at its start and running
  to the window's edge, where Material's pane scaffold gives a pane 360 dp
  (412 dp from large up) with a 24 dp spacer.
- **The sheet.** Faraday's sheets stand centred (the window's width less a
  margin on compact, at most 560 dp otherwise), not as Material's bottom
  sheet on compact and modal side sheet on medium and expanded.
- **The bar's button** is labelled **Your choices** as the owner named it,
  where Material's trailing actions are icon buttons.

# F319 Your choices stands at the left in every flow; in a flow begun beside the session pane it is the pane's side (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Not built. `docs/WORKSPACE.md`'s Open line says what is left.*

*Would amend F296 point 3 (Your choices at the right), its "pane's width and look" (31% of the page area, 240 to 320 dp, a flat pane with a divider) and, through F318, point 2 (the 300 ms). Differs from Material 3, whose supporting pane stands at the trailing edge (Canonical layouts, Supporting pane): here Your choices leads, since it is the same pane the person picked the key in.*

*At a flow's last step the session pane turns into the take-away (F320), so Your choices stands at the left in every step but the last.*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-21): in a flow, the session pane's side of the screen should become the flow's Your choices, so one pane runs on from Home to a menu page to the flow: the picked key is its first row, each answer adds a row, and a press on a row goes back to its step.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21).

1. **Your choices at the left in every flow**, also in a flow not begun beside the pane (Make a new vault from Wallets start). It is the session pane in its Your choices form (F316), the pane's look off Home in place of the flat Your choices pane it was, at the pane's width (`LIST_PANE`, 360 dp), narrowing to `CHOICES_PANE_MIN` (240 dp) only after the flow's column has narrowed to `FLOW_CARD_MIN`.
2. **In a flow begun beside the pane**, the session pane is Your choices: the pick (or the wallet a flow names, as Sign a transaction does) is its first row ("Key: Savings"), each answer a row under it, a press on one going back to its step on F296's rules; the key row brings the picker back. The wallet chip at the flow card's head goes.
3. **The guide is the third pane**, level with the flow's pair, which slides left where the guide needs the room; a sheet where there is no room. It stays open across pages and flows, with no pin (owner, 2026-11-22).
4. **The phone-size window and the Pi** keep the top app bar's Your choices button and its sheet (F296 point 4).
5. What the pane holds under its rows (Sign a transaction's "This transaction", Back up's map) moves to the left with it. The backup board's list is its step's Your choices, at the left, in one card with Places (owner, 2026-11-22).
6. Entering a flow from a stacked page turns the layout into the flow's pair (owner, 2026-11-22): one place for Your choices in every flow. The pair is one card with an inset divider (F324).

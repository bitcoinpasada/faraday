# F319 Your choices stands at the left in every flow; in a flow begun beside the session card it is the card's side (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Proposed
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Not built. `docs/WORKSPACE.md`'s Open line says what is left.*

*Would amend F296 point 3 (Your choices at the right), its "pane's width and look" (31% of the page area, 240 to 320 dp, a flat pane with a divider) and, through F318, point 2 (the 300 ms). Differs from Material 3, whose supporting pane stands at the trailing edge (Canonical layouts, Supporting pane): here Your choices leads, since it is the same pane the person picked the key in.*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-21): in a flow, the session card's side of the screen should become the flow's Your choices, so one pane runs on from Home to a menu page to the flow: the picked key is its first row, each answer adds a row, and a press on a row goes back to its step.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21).

1. **Your choices at the left in every flow**, also in a flow not begun beside the card (Make a new vault from Wallets start). It is the session card in its Your choices form (F316), the card's look off Home in place of a flat pane, at the card's width (`LIST_PANE`, 360 dp), narrowing to `CHOICES_PANE_MIN` (240 dp) only after the flow's column has narrowed to `FLOW_CARD_MIN`.
2. **In a flow begun beside the card**, the card is the pane: the pick (or the wallet a flow names, as Sign a transaction does) is its first row ("Key: Savings"), each answer a row under it, a press on one going back to its step on F296's rules; the key row brings the picker back. The wallet chip at the flow card's head goes.
3. **Guide's pane stays at the right** on extra-large windows, beside the flow; the room it needs does not change.
4. **The phone-size window and the Pi** keep the top app bar's Your choices button and its sheet (F296 point 4).
5. What the pane holds under its rows (Sign a transaction's "This transaction", Back up's map) moves to the left with it. The backup board's list on its full-width step stays in its own pane at the right.
6. With the card above the page, it glides to the left as the flow takes it and becomes Your choices (owner, 2026-11-21): one place for Your choices in every flow.

# F123 A secret shows at a press on its eye, never by a hold (2026-10-26)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** keys

**Why.** Vault contents showed a key's words, its SeedQR, an entry's
password and TOTP secret, a note and a paperkey only while a finger or
the mouse was held on them. People know the eye: a press shows, a press
hides.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-26; DESIGN §3, §4.10, §5 VaultContents,
`docs/FLOWS.md`, `docs/QR.md` §7, `docs/NOSTR.md` §5). No secret is
shown by a hold: every masked secret (a vault key's words and SeedQR, an
entry's password, TOTP secret and notes, a note, a paperkey) shows at a press
or tap on its eye and hides at the next. It hides again once its page is
left (another screen, vault, kind or record, a form or panel in its
place, a sheet over it), and at a lock or the idle lock. The TOTP
secret's code is opened by a press on Show as QR. Holds that confirm an
act (sign, delete, wipe, revoke, power off, restart) stay.

This amends F7 and F28, whose TOTP secret was shown by a hold.

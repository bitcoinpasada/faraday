# F331 Everything raised rests at one level; in a flow the flow's card alone is raised, and the search and the pills stay (2026-11-24)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** review

*Not built. `docs/WORKSPACE.md`'s Open line says what is left.*

*Differs from Material 3 (Elevation: https://m3.material.io/styles/elevation/overview): there an elevated card rests at level 1, as here, but a standard navigation drawer and a text or tonal button rest at level 0, and a component's level changes for its own state (hover, drag, scroll), not because the page turned into a flow; level 3, there a dialog's and a floating button's, is a card's here. The page itself is read again in the build. The levels, the shadows and what floats (menus, dialogs, sheets, the toast) are Material's. Would amend F315 point 3 (level 3), F316 point 2 (a level lower than Home's), F317 point 2 (the only raised card), F320 point 2 (the take-away's own level 1), F323 point 5 and F324 point 1 (the outline, no fill).*

**Why.** One resting level reads as one surface at rest, and one raised card shows that a flow is the only thing under way. The search and the pills staying means less movement.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-24).

1. The search, the three pills, This session (on Home too), the sidebar and the tiles rest at level 1. Menus, dialogs, sheets, the toast, the notice and the phone's Scan button keep their levels; a menu is still drawn over the flow's card of point 2. The phone-size window and the Pi do not change.
2. Entering a flow, in the movement in which the two panes become one card, that card rises to level 3 with a fill of its own (surface-container-low, to be looked at in the build) and everything else that had a height drops to none. Leaving puts it back. With Motion Off both are at once.
3. The flow's card has no outline (F324 point 1), except at High contrast.
4. The search and the pills go flat and are never dimmed. They stay in a flow where they fit beside its heading, after the bar's own shrinking (the field, then the Start button), and are left out only where that does not fit. They are held off wherever the sidebar is.
5. A press on a pill in a flow works as the sidebar's press does, the flow kept in progress. The pill of the flow on screen is off: New wallet never starts a second one inside the first.
6. A search typed in a flow shows a page with the results alone, every height at level 1, and does not leave the flow: Escape or the cross goes back to the same step with what was typed. Only a press on a result leaves it.

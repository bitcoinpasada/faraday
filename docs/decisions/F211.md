# F211 The QR vault's judges: PyCA's primitives, sq reads only and shares Argon2, rPGP the only outside sealer (2026-11-04)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** crypto
**Held by:** crypto.crosscheck-sources

*Amends F190 point 11 and F193 point 1, for the QR vault only, and F192 point 6 for rPGP's program.*

**Why.** F193 point 1 names Debian's `sq`, over Nettle, as the judge of the
OpenPGP primitives, and F190 point 11 has it open and write files both ways.
Building the area showed that neither holds. Sequoia's Argon2 S2K is the
RustCrypto `argon2` crate on every backend, Nettle included (read in its
manifest and `s2k.rs`), so for Argon2 it is another version of the app's own
library, which F192 does not count. `sq` 1.3.1 writes a SKESK v4 and a SEIPD
v1 for a password under either profile, so it cannot seal a QR vault.

**Decision** (2026-11-04). In the F79 area `qrvault`:

1. PyCA cryptography (OpenSSL) judges the primitives: Argon2id, HKDF-SHA-256
   and AES-256-OCB, beneath an OpenPGP framing written in the area. It opens
   every seal of Faraday's, refusing anything that is not exactly the QR
   vault's form, and seals messages Faraday opens. The framing is the area's
   own and is not called an outside check.
2. `sq`, over Nettle, opens Faraday's messages and judges AES and OCB and
   HKDF that way. It does not judge Argon2, and it seals nothing; the area
   asserts that Faraday refuses what it writes, so a later `sq` that can seal
   stops the run and is taken up.
3. rPGP judges the format only (F193 point 1) and is the only outside program
   that seals a QR vault.
4. CPython's `tomllib` reads each item beside the comparison (F193 point 3).
5. rPGP's program is pinned by its `Cargo.lock` and inside `cargo vet`,
   unlike what F192 point 6 allows for a Rust program (pinned by commit and
   SHA-256, outside `cargo vet`); this amends F192 point 6 for this program.
   It is outside the root workspace only because it has a `[workspace]` of
   its own.

KDBX's judges and the rest of F193 stay as they are.

**Not done.** No program of another lineage seals a QR vault whole: on the
open path the primitives are judged through point 1's framing, and the format
through rPGP. pysequoia and OpenPGP.js stay as F193 point 2 says.

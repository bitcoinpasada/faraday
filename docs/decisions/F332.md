# F332 What can be pressed shows it: a chevron opens, a radio or a box chooses, an icon and a verb act, and a fact looks like none of them (2026-11-24)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** review

*Not built. `docs/WORKSPACE.md` §19 says where it applies and which of it a lint is to hold.*

*Differs from Material 3: a list item there may have a trailing icon and no page says when (Lists: https://m3.material.io/components/lists/guidelines); here a row that opens always ends in a chevron and no other row does. Buttons there have no style for a dangerous act and the error roles are for errors (Buttons: https://m3.material.io/components/buttons/guidelines); here the first press of one is in the error tone, and the last is a hold (F136), which Material does not have. Material has no status tag; its outlined chips are presses (Chips: https://m3.material.io/components/chips/guidelines), as an outline in the accent is here. One filled button a step is stricter than Material's advice to use few. The pages are read again in the build. Would amend nothing; F111's one leading action a step stands, and the network pill (F81) gains a sign.*

**Why.** Across the app a row that opens has a chevron in some places and not in others, some facts have a button's outline or colour, and some presses have no sign. One sign for each job lets a person see what can be pressed before pressing it.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-24).

1. **Opens: a chevron.** What opens a page or details ends in a chevron: a row, a line, a chart's box. What opens a menu or the mode sheet from a button or a pill has the small downward arrow. The chevron at the end of a row has one size.
2. **Chooses: a radio or a box.** A row that is chosen has a radio where one is taken, a tick box where several are, and no chevron. The segmented button, the switch and the chips keep their own signs.
3. **Acts: an icon and a verb.** A row that acts where it stands has an icon and a verb, and no chevron. A button has its verb.
4. **A fact reacts to nothing.** It has no outline in the accent and no text in the accent: those are for presses. A status is a neutral tag.
5. **At most one filled button a step.** A second action that matters is tonal.
6. **A dangerous act's first press is in the error tone**, whatever its kind (a button, a row, a menu's row). Its last press stays a hold.
7. The tonal button and what is chosen share one blue fill; this stays.

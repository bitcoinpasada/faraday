# F287 A second explanation on a working screen: the info button and tooltip after BIP-48 on Make a new wallet's path row (2026-11-18)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** strings.no-explanation

**Why.** A working screen carries labels, values and actions, never an
explanation (CLAUDE.md). A person choosing a multisig path on Make a new
wallet's quorum card meets two names, BIP-48 and BIP-87, and one fact
tells them which is the usual one: BIP-48 is what most wallets use.
That fact is short enough to say where the choice is made.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-18).

- Right after "BIP-48" on the multisig path row, Material's info icon
  button (as F112's: `Icon::Info` in an `ICON_BUTTON` round, an
  `ICON_SMALL` glyph in the secondary ink, a `TOUCH` target taking the
  press only on the button) opens a rich tooltip: "BIP-48 is the
  standard path for a multisig wallet, the one most wallets use. BIP-87
  is a newer path, the same for every script type; fewer wallets support
  it."
- It is the rich tooltip on the info button of F112: it opens at once
  on a press, stays until the next press or Escape, and also shows on
  the pointer or keyboard resting on the button and a finger's long
  press.
- The button stands after the closed value when it is BIP-48, and after
  the segmented button once Change is pressed, never between two
  segments. The made wallet's row and the legacy multisig's row (`m/45'`,
  for which the text is not true) have none.
- It is a deliberate exception to the working-screen rule, in this one
  place, besides F112's. Another explanation on a working screen needs a
  decision of its own.

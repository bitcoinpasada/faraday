# F111 The Material 3 components and patterns Faraday leaves out (2026-10-24)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design

**Why.** Faraday follows Material 3 (F91, F108), and a difference
without a decision is a defect (CLAUDE.md). Some of Material's
components and patterns do not fit a device that signs offline, is
worked by touch on a 2.8-inch panel as by mouse on a monitor, and must
never let a person act on a string they did not see whole. This
records each one left out, so its absence is a decision and not a gap.

**Decision.**

- **An action or a decision on the snackbar.** The toast says what a
  press did and goes after 4 s (DESIGN §4.11). An action on it can be
  gone before a person reaches it; a choice that matters gets a sheet
  that waits.
- **Swipe actions.** A swipe hides its action: nothing on the row says
  it is there, and with a mouse it is a drag.
  Every row's actions are its ⋮ menu or a button that shows.
- **Split button and FAB menu.** Each hides choices behind a second
  press on one control. A step leads with one primary action, its
  others beside it as buttons (DESIGN §4.13); the floating Scan opens
  the scanner and nothing else.
- **Carousel.** It shows part of what it holds and moves it out of
  view. What Faraday lists (keys, wallets, files, places) is read in
  full, in a list or a grid that scrolls.
- **Truncating addresses or amounts in lists, search results or
  titles.** Material cuts a long line at its end. A cut address,
  amount or fingerprint can read as another one; Faraday shows the
  string whole where it is read or checked, and the short form, which
  keeps both ends, where a line only names it (DESIGN §4.5).
- **Calendar and dial pickers.** The one date and time Faraday asks for
  is the clock it sets for TOTP and GPG (F7, `docs/TOTP.md`), typed as
  digits that the panel's keyboard and a physical keyboard both enter;
  a picker adds pages of presses for one value.
- **Pull to refresh.** Nothing on the device is fetched: what a screen
  shows is the state as it is, drawn again on each change.
- **Loading indicator and wavy progress.** Material 3 Expressive's
  loading indicator and wavy linear indicator move all the time, which
  costs frames on the Pi and is lost under reduce motion; Faraday keeps
  the flat linear indicator (DESIGN §4.14).
- **Elevated and filled cards, modal drawer and bottom app bar.**
  Already decided: one page colour and steps as sections, not cards
  (F39); the expanded rail in place of a drawer and the top app bar
  holding Learn, Settings and Lock (`docs/LAYOUT.md` §10, calls 3
  and 5).

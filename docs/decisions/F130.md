# F130 The boot import reads names and sizes until Import, then only the ticked files (2026-10-27)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** stick
**Held by:** tests

**Why.** F60's boot sheet came up after Faraday had copied every file
on the boot stick into memory at its first look, and showed what each
held (its wallets, keys and seeds, a PSBT count) before the person had
said yes to any of it. Nothing read was used until Import, but a file
the person would decline had already been read. The owner prefers the
stick to stay unread until the person chooses: there is no known
danger in reading first, and not reading is the cleaner rule.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-27; amends F60; F62's Load sheet after
each unlock is unchanged; `docs/FLOWS.md` Session: Boot).

- At the first look at the boot stick in a power-on, nothing on it is
  read but its listing: each file's name and size, as the disk process
  gives them for any stick. The sheet lists every file by name and
  size, each ticked, with what it is from its name alone, said as a
  guess ("PSBT, by its name · 1.2 KB"), and one line counting them the
  same way ("1 vault · 1 PSBT · 5 other files, by their names").
- **Import** asks the stick for the ticked files and for no other. While
  they are read the sheet lists them and says how many are read ("3 of
  5 files read"), its ticks fixed and Import not offered. Once every one
  is answered, what they held comes in as F60 says: the files into
  Files, their wallets and keys through Files' loader, a seed with the
  stick in loading as it is pulled, the vaults into Files, locked, each
  then offered its Unlock and its Load sheet (F62). The counts of
  wallets, keys and files are said then, in the toast "Imported 1
  wallet · 2 keys · 5 files".
- The stick pulled while they are read: what was read comes in, and the
  toast adds how many were not read ("· 1 not read").
- **Later**, or a tap beside the sheet, reads nothing and brings in
  nothing; Home leads with the import as before.
- The stick pulled before Import: the sheet stays as it is, Import is not
  offered, and it says "Put the stick back in to import" (Pi: the SD
  card). Put back while the import waits, the boot stick brings the
  sheet up again over Home, ticks as they were, instead of a stick
  visit.
- On the Pi, SD card for stick, as everywhere.

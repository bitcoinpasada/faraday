# F149 One registry of what a change brings with it, checked at landing and before the website publishes (2026-10-28)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** repository
**Held by:** impact.registry, impact.rules-indexed, decisions.held-by

**Why.** The rules that say "a change to this needs that too" are
written in `CLAUDE.md`, in decisions, in documents' preambles and in
the lints' own comments. About a third are held by a lint in `just
lint`; the rest are held by a reviewer who remembers them. Nothing lists
them in one place, so a task's brief cannot say what its change will
need, a landing cannot tell a pair left unmet, and the website, which
reads the strings, the guide, the builds list and the colour roles of a
pinned commit, learns of a change only when someone notices it.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-28; `docs/IMPACT.md`).

1. **One registry.** tools/impact.toml holds every rule of the form "a
   change to these paths: these checks hold it, these files change with
   it, these parts of the suite read it", each with what holds it and
   what nothing catches. The suite is the apps and the website.
2. **One script.** tools/impact.py, Python's standard library only,
   says at planning what a change to some paths brings with it, in a
   few lines for a brief; at landing, in `just lint`, fails on a pair
   left unmet in the range from `main`; and, run by the website on the
   range between the commit it last published and its new pin, says
   which of the website's parts are not yet brought up to date, and the
   website's publish refuses while a blocking one is not.
3. **A waiver says why.** A commit that does not need a pair carries
   `Impact-skip: <rule>: <reason>`. There is no other way past.
   A change to a screen or a component names the Material 3 page it
   was checked against in a `Material: <page>` trailer (F91, F92); a
   landing without it fails like a pair left unmet.
4. **The list of checks is generated.** docs/CHECKS.md is written from
   the registry and never by hand; `just lint` fails while they differ.
5. **One extension point.** Further rule files named in the environment
   variable `FARADAY_IMPACT_EXTRA` are read after the registry. The
   repository names none and holds none.
6. **Every rule has a home.** A decision that binds later changes says
   which rules hold it, in a `**Held by:**` line, or says `review`,
   `tests` or `none`; `lint-docs` holds the line. A new rule line in
   `CLAUDE.md` is a new rule in the registry in the same commit.

**Where:** `docs/IMPACT.md`; tools/impact.toml and tools/impact.py (to
be written); `tools/lint-docs.sh` (rule 6); `justfile` (`lint`).

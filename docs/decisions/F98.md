# F98 A new wallet's account is asked after its quorum, not on Kind (2026-10-23)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** wallets
**Held by:** tests

**Why.** F55 put Make a new wallet's **Account** stepper on Kind, the
first card, because the account is part of the kind's standard path.
Most wallets are made at account 0, and nothing in F55 needs the account
asked first; the person met a number to decide before choosing anything
else.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-22). Amends F55: Account leaves Kind.

- The account is asked after the quorum and before the keys, since Keys'
  Continue makes the wallet: at the foot of the Quorum card, below the
  quorum's line, or, for a kind with no Quorum card, at the head of Keys.
  A kind with no account (`m/45'`, the shares dealt here) has no row.
- It is a value row, **Account** and its number, with **Change**, which
  opens the − n + stepper in its place. The number starts where it did:
  0 for a wallet made afresh, and for **New wallet from this one** the
  lowest account no loaded wallet of the same keys and kind uses
  (`docs/FLOWS.md`).
- The closed line of the card that carries the row names the account
  ("2 of 3 · account 1") where the wallet is made from another or at an
  account other than 0; Kind's line is the kind alone.
- Check's Path **Change** takes the made wallet back and opens the card
  that carries the row, with its stepper open. Everything else in F55
  stands.

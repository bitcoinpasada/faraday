# F163 The toast's text is centred (2026-10-29)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** review

**Why.** Material 3's snackbar sets its supporting text from its start.
Faraday's toasts are one short fact with no action ("USB Receiver is in
use"), and on a window wider than compact the snackbar is at least
344 dp wide, so a short line from its start leaves most of the container
empty on one side, which reads as a fault rather than as a message.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-29). Every toast (`Ui::snackbar`,
`docs/DESIGN.md` §4.11, `docs/THEMES.md` §7) centres each of its lines
in the container. Everything else stays Material's: the inverse-surface
container and inverse-on-surface text, body medium, extra-small corners,
elevation level 3, the compact window's width less its margins or else
as wide as its text between 344 and 672 dp, wrapping at the widest, and
standing above a floating action button in its way.

# F268 Decisions that no longer bind move to history (2026-11-14)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** repository
**Held by:** review

*Amends F158 (2026-11-14): its point 4, the vault's four-slot container, no longer binds and is cut to one line naming F190; the rest of F158 holds.*

**Why.** Of the decisions under `docs/decisions/`, many no longer tell
anyone changing Faraday what to do. Some had every point replaced by
later decisions (the Pi's images, the vault's earlier formats, the
desktop app's names and releases). Some only record a count that a
`.counts` file now holds, or a count whose lines have since fallen out
of it. The rest of what they say is held by a Reference, a Spec's
design or a lint. A reader opening the index to find what binds a
change reads all of them to learn that.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-14).

1. **The rule,** as `CLAUDE.md` states it in its decisions bullet: a
   decision stays under `docs/decisions/` only while it binds later
   changes and no Reference, Spec or lint holds all that it says. One
   whose every point is replaced, which only records a count a
   `.counts` file holds, or whose feature is unbuilt, moves to
   `docs/history/decisions/` and keeps its row. A difference from
   Material 3 keeps its decision live, since such a difference needs
   one.
2. **The statuses of a moved decision.** **Withdrawn** where its
   feature is unbuilt or taken out; **Superseded by F<n>** where later
   decisions, or a document or lint named by this decision, now hold
   what it says; **Replaced by F<n>** keeps its meaning (a later
   decision takes its place).

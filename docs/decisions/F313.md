# F313 Home's page stays still and only its box scrolls; the rail's Home has no place tiles (2026-11-21)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** tests

*Differs from Material 3, where a list's page scrolls with it (Lists, https://m3.material.io/components/lists/guidelines): a list scrolling within its container is Material's, a page whose top stays still is the difference.*

**Why.** The owner (2026-11-21): with many rows the whole of Home scrolled and the search bar and first actions left the screen.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-21).

1. On the sidebar windows and the rail's windows Home's page does not scroll. The search bar and the three first actions stay at the top; under them the box of list rows (one-off rows, All keys, at most three key rows, which drop first as the window gets short) grows down to a page margin above the window's foot and scrolls within itself.
2. The rail's Home draws no place tiles (Wallets, Files, Vaults, Tools, Settings): the rail lists every one.
3. The phone-size window and the Pi's menu are unchanged.

Described in `docs/HOME.md` §3, §6.2 and §7.3.

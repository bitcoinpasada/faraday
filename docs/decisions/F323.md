# F323 Home's card shows everything in the session, in groups; its box scrolls when too short (2026-11-22)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** review

*Built for Home's card (`docs/SESSION-PANE.md`): the groups, every key and wallet, the Waiting rows with their buttons, the columns and the scrolling box. Built since: the top bar's search and pills (point 4, `screens/bar_search.rs`). Not built: the card's place on the other pages; `docs/WORKSPACE.md`'s Open line says what is left.*

*Differs from Material 3 as F315 does (level 3 on an elevated card). Would amend F313 point 1, F314 (the row above the card, the icons on its buttons) and F315 points 1, 2 and 4. F331 (not built) would amend point 5: level 1. The owner's calls of 2026-11-24 (not built, `docs/WORKSPACE.md` §1) would amend points 1, 2, 3 and 6: Keys lead, and Waiting is a band at the card's foot, outside the part that scrolls.*

**Why.** The pane's place no longer depends on how much is loaded, so Home's card can grow to show everything; one list of mixed rows does not separate what is waiting from what is loaded.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-22).

1. Home's card holds three groups under small headings: Waiting, Wallets (Backups last), Keys. Every loaded wallet and every key has a row, a wallet's need in orange, a key a wallet uses naming it. There is no All keys row, no cap on key rows, no Make a wallet button, and no Make or Restore row (F315 points 2 and 4).
2. On a wide card the groups stand in columns, as many as fit. The PSBT is a one-column item, its Sign button under its two lines at the text's start.
3. The card grows as long as its content. On a window too short for it, its own box scrolls and nothing is dropped (F313 point 1's key rows dropping first goes). The card is centred directly under the top bar, at the height of This session on every other page.
4. The search and the three first actions stand in the top bar, the actions as text-only pills, on Home only; the search on every page that has one (F314).
5. F315 point 3 (level 3, neutral fill, orange only on what needs something) stands.
6. The Waiting group always shows the locked vault file with Unlock, the last write ("Written to {stick}"), an import put off with Later, and a vault changed since it was written, and one row per waiting PSBT, each with its own Sign, one Unlock row per locked vault file, and every lead's button (Carry on, Finish, Unlock, Write, Open) under its two lines at the text's start, in the Waiting group and one column (owner, 2026-11-23).
7. With nothing loaded the card stands at its place, holding what is real of point 6, and with none of it the one line "Nothing loaded"; it has no Make or Restore row (owner, 2026-11-23).

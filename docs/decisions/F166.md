# F166 A BIP-85 child seed's parent is kept only in a vault, when the person records it; nothing outside one says so; a wallet made over one says so once (2026-10-29)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** keys
**Held by:** keys.child-vault-only

**Why.** A BIP-85 child seed gives nothing away about the key it comes
from: its words, its fingerprint and the wallets over it are those of
any other seed. Whoever finds its backup cannot tell that another key
exists unless something written beside it says so, and that is what
lets a person deny the parent. Faraday used to name a child seed after
its parent ("BIP-85 Words · 3dca6805 · index 0") as its label, and a
label goes where a key's name goes: a vanity wallet's name and from
there its files and printed sheets, the key's passphrase keys, and the
child words' own file out of BIP-85 (`bip85-3dca6805-words-0.txt`, its
path inside). The person may also want no record of the relation at all,
in the vault or anywhere.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-29).

1. **The person chooses.** Before child words go into a vault (**Save
   into …**) or load as a key (**Load as a key**), the dialog **Record it
   as a child seed?** asks, its two actions **Don't record** and
   **Record** alike, neither the default; a press beside it or Escape puts
   the words nowhere. Recorded, the key carries the parent's fingerprint,
   the application (39) and the index: in the vault's key record, field 6
   (`docs/VAULT.md` §7), and in the session while it is loaded, written
   into every vault the key is saved into or copied to, and read back at
   every load. Not recorded, the vault and the session hold an ordinary
   seed, with nothing that ties it to its parent, and Faraday treats it
   as one from then on. A record is changed only by removing the key
   from the vault and putting it in again: the record format has no
   edit of one field, and none is added.
2. **The vault is the only place it is kept.** A child seed is named by
   its own fingerprint; its label is never the mark. Keys (the sidebar,
   Explore's and BIP-85's key rows, the vault's line for its record)
   shows a recorded child seed as "BIP-85 Words · 3dca6805 · index 0",
   drawn from the record, never written anywhere. Nothing written for a
   seed's backup or out of Faraday (its words on paper or in a file, a
   SeedQR, codex32 strings, Seed XOR parts, a wallet's files, names and
   sheets, a backup plan, a file name, the state a lock keeps) names its
   parent, says BIP-85 or carries its index or path. Child words out
   unprotected go as a seed's words file, `<fingerprint>-words.txt`,
   holding the words alone.
3. **A notice at wallet creation.** A wallet made in Create over a key
   recorded as a child seed opens **A BIP-85 child seed**: Faraday
   records that the key is a child seed only inside a vault, nothing
   written for its backup says where it came from, and to remember that
   the person writes it down. One action, **OK**; once for each wallet
   made. A key not recorded gives no notice. Learn's Seed words page,
   Keys and other secrets from one key, says why.

Not covered: a password, WIF, extended private key or hex out of BIP-85
is not a seed. Its vault entry or note keeps its title and path, which
name the parent, and its file out unprotected keeps its name and path,
since the path and the parent are what make it again.

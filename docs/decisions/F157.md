# F157 Wherever the system's randomness is used, the person can add their own: Add randomness at every press that draws it (2026-10-28)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** security
**Held by:** review

**Why.** F86 offered Add randomness in Settings and on New key's This
device only. What a person adds is already mixed into every draw of
the session (`docs/OWN-RANDOMNESS.md` §2.3), but nothing at a vault's
Create, a GPG key's Make, a Secure Boot signature, a share split or a
MuSig2 round says so or offers it. A person who does not trust the
system's generator has to know to go to Settings first.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-28; the spec is `docs/OWN-RANDOMNESS.md` §8).

1. **The rule.** Wherever a cryptographic function would just use the
   system's randomness, the person can add their own. This holds for
   seed generation too: New key's This device mixes in what was added
   (already F86).
2. **Where.** Add randomness is offered at every press that draws the
   system's randomness (§8.1's list), and in Settings. The step or pane
   that holds the press carries the Randomness row: the status line and
   **Add randomness**, which opens the existing page and comes back to
   the press (F77). On the Pi's panel the action is the first row of the
   bar's ⋮ and the step keeps the line.
3. **Never in the way.** The press is never blocked or delayed by it,
   and nothing asks for an addition. No source starts until the person
   picks one (owner, 2026-10-26: nothing is collected passively).
4. **Not for the person's own sources.** Keys from Dice, Coins or Cards
   are the person's own randomness: they take none of the system's and
   have no Add randomness at their press.
5. **The mixing is unchanged.** What is added is hashed with the
   system's bytes, never used alone (§2.3). The Starter draws no key
   material and offers none.

Amends F86's "Add randomness is offered in Settings and New key only".

**Where:** `docs/OWN-RANDOMNESS.md` §2.2, §5 item 3, §8;
`faraday/faraday-core/src/fresh.rs`,

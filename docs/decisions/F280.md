# F280 Unlocking a vault from the Vaults list lands in the vault (2026-11-17)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** vault
**Held by:** tests

*Amends F62 (2026-10-20): where its Vaults tab unlock landed on Files, it lands in the vault; the Load sheet and the rest of F62 hold.*

**Why.** A person who unlocks a vault from the list wants to see what it
holds. Landing on Files, with the Load sheet over it, or only a toast
when the vault holds entries or notes, sent them away from it; a vault
just made already opens in its contents.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-11-17).

- An unlock from the Vaults list lands on that vault's contents, with
  the Load sheet over it where F62 shows one. **Load ticked** and
  **Not now** leave the person there.
- An unlock a flow asked for returns to the flow (decision 10); the
  boot sheet's landing is F60's. Neither changes.

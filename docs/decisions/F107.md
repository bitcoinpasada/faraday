# F107 Single key, backup in shares (owner, 2026-10-22)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** wallets
**Held by:** review

**Why.** Codex32 gives a single-key wallet much of what a person reaches
for multisig to get on the backup side: no one paper or place loses the
wallet, and fewer than k papers show nothing of it. It does not do what
multisig does at spending: to sign, the whole key is on one device. So
the choice belongs where the person chooses what kind of wallet to make,
as an answer of its own whose row states plainly what it is
(`docs/CODEX32.md` §6.9).

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-22).

- Make a new wallet's Kind card offers **Single key, backup in shares**
  between Single key and Multisig, its second line a fact of the row
  ("One key, its backup in 3 shares, any 2 rebuild it", the counts the
  scheme's). How the kinds compare is Learn's.
- On chain it is a single-key native SegWit wallet, as Single key is:
  one `wpkh` descriptor, one xpub to a coordinator. Restore does not
  offer it.
- Its Quorum asks the codex32 scheme: how many to rebuild it (2 to 9)
  and how many shares (k to 31); "None, the secret alone" is not
  offered here. The Account row follows at Quorum's foot (F98).
- Its one slot opens Make codex32 shares with the scheme fixed, starting
  at the identifier; Lock in fills the slot and makes the wallet. Check
  names the set ("Backup · codex32 w0za · 2 of 3"); Back up opens on
  codex32's standard way, one string per place, on paper (§9.5).
- Codex32 only: SLIP-39 stays under New key's Other forms.
- Codex32 is made for single-key wallets only: this kind's slot, Single
  key's slot and a key on its own. A slot of a multisig, Taproot
  multisig, MuSig2 or recovery wallet offers BIP-39 words only
  under Make a new key. A codex32 key already loaded (typed, scanned,
  from a vault) is refused nowhere: it fills any slot a key can.

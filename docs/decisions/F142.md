# F142 A stick's pill is filled in its tone while the stick is in, its tones set by mode and contrast (2026-10-27)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design

**Why.** The sticks' pills (the boot stick's orange, then a hue for
each backup stick) are drawn outside the schemes, chosen by Mode
(`docs/THEMES.md` §6), but their text still falls under THEMES §11.2.
The second Material 3 theming check (2026-10-27) measured a pill whose
stick is in, its label in its tone on a see-through tint of the same
tone: 3.44 to 4.98:1 in Light, five of eight tones under 4.5:1 at
every contrast, 2.86 to 3.99:1 while pressed, and none at 7:1 at High
contrast. The tint, a fixed colour, also escaped High contrast's rule
that a tint under text is drawn as its edge. Darkening the tones until
their own text read on their tint took the Dark set to near white at
Medium and High contrast, the hues lost.

**Decision.** A stick's pill (`faraday_ui::stick`) is drawn as
Material's chips are, with nothing see-through in it:

- **In**: filled in the stick's tone, its label and dot in white on a
  light page's tones and black on a dark page's (`pal::on_stick`).
- **Asked for**: surface-container-highest, edged in its tone, its
  label in on-surface, its dot in its tone.
- **Quiet**: surface-container-highest, edged in outline-variant, its
  label in on-surface-variant, its dot in its tone.
- **Pressed**: the state layer of its label's colour, as every control.

The tones are a theme's colours (`pal::Paint::Stick`), so the High
contrast rules apply to them as to a role, and they are set by mode and
contrast: on a light page the light set taken down until white on each
reads 4.5:1 (7:1 at High contrast, a darker set); on a dark page the
dark set as it was, its red, blue, violet and magenta lightened at High
contrast until black on each reads 7:1. Every tone is 3:1 or more
against every surface role in every scheme, so which stick a pill is
reads from its tone. Each pill's label reads 4.5:1 or more in every
scheme and 7:1 at High contrast, tested on the frame
(`faraday/faraday-ui/src/read_tests.rs`).

Amends THEMES §6's "chosen by Mode alone": the sticks' tones are chosen
by mode and contrast, still outside the schemes.

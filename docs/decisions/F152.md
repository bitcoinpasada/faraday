# F152 Touch targets are tested over the snapshot tours, and a press cannot be made without its state layer (2026-10-28)

**Kind:** Record
**Status:** Active
**Area:** design
**Held by:** design.touch-targets, design.press-layers

**Why.** F108 says a press is taken on at least 48 × 48 dp on every
layout and that hover, focus and press are Material's state layers; both
are held by review alone. A measuring run over eight snapshot tours found
157 screen and control kinds whose target is under 48 dp, and 10 of the
56 controls that draw their own state look draw none, so review has not
held them.

**Decision** (owner, 2026-10-28; `docs/M3-CHECKS.md` §2 to §4, §8). The touch-target test holds its first part; `design.press-layers` holds its second part: the press constructors are built and the `hit*`, `state_layer` and `focus_ring` functions are private to faraday-ui.

- **Touch targets are tested.** A test over the snapshot tours, at four
  sizes, reports every press target under `TOUCH` that is not cut by a
  clip, naming the size, tour, step, screen, sheet and control. What is
  under the floor when the test lands is counted in
  tools/touch-targets.counts, which only falls; raising a count is the
  owner's decision, recorded here.
- **A split target counts.** F108's sharing of the gap between two
  controls stays, so a press never reaches the wrong one; a target cut
  under the floor by it is still a defect, cured by room between the
  controls.
- **48 dp on every layout**, the desktop's included.
- **Exceptions are listed.** A target allowed under the floor is a row of
  `docs/M3-CHECKS.md` §3 naming its case and size classes. Allowed: the
  on-screen keyboard's and word entry's keys on the 240 × 320 and
  480 × 640 panels, the codex32 worksheet's cells, and the backup
  board's chips (already allowed by F108). A new row is the owner's
  decision, recorded here.
- **State layers by construction.** A pressable region is made only
  through faraday-ui's press functions, which draw its hover, focus and
  pressed layers and its focus ring in one call; the layer and ring
  cannot be drawn apart from a press, nor a press made without them. A
  press with no layer names one of the listed cases (beside a sheet, a
  menu's or sheet's own body, the page itself); a press with hover and
  focus but no pressed layer names one too (a held secret, an on-screen
  keyboard's key). A new case is the owner's decision, recorded here.
- **The keyboard's keys show no press**, hover and focus kept, so a
  watcher reads nothing of what is typed from a highlight: Faraday's
  difference from Material's pressed state.

**Amends** F108: a split target under the floor is a defect, the
exceptions are listed, the states are drawn by construction, and the
keyboard's keys show no pressed state.

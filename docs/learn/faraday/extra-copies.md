# Extra copies

## Copies outside the plan

A backup plan keeps at most one place for each key of the wallet. A copy kept anywhere else is an extra copy. Faraday does not track extra copies, and the plan's checks do not count them: "Any one place lost", "One place found: can spend" and "One place found: sees the balance" are worked out from the plan's places alone.

Extra copies are made once the wallet is made and its backup is done.

## A vault

A vault is one file on a stick. Copy between sticks, in a stick visit, copies the vault's file to another stick. The copy opens with the same passphrase and holds the same records. It does not change when the vault on the first stick is written again: a later change is copied again to keep the two the same.

## Words on paper

A seed's words are copied by hand from the paper they are on, onto a new blank sheet, in order, and checked word by word against the first. A SeedQR is copied the same way, square by square.

## The wallet description

The wallet description is saved again from the wallet's card: its text or its QR picture to a stick, or printed. A share of the description is saved the same way from the backup's checklist.

## Each copy is as sensitive as what it copies

A copy of a seed's words spends the wallet as the words do, with the passphrase where the seed has one. A copy of a vault is opened by the vault's passphrase. A copy of the description shows the wallet's balance and every address. Each copy needs the same care and a place of its own, kept as the plan keeps the first.

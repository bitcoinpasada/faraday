Faraday Companion is an Android app with no network access. It reads the codes a Faraday device shows, shows codes for the device to read, and keeps what arrives.

<b>What it does</b>
<ul>
<li>Reads a Faraday device's QR codes, single and animated, and keeps each whole file.</li>
<li>Shows a file, a PSBT or a wallet description as QR codes for the device to read.</li>
<li>Takes a file shared from another app, and shares a kept file to one.</li>
<li>Keeps wallet descriptions with each one's first address.</li>
<li>Carries Faraday's Learn pages, and opens the Starter's guide addresses as its own pages.</li>
</ul>

<b>What it never does</b>
<ul>
<li>Takes seed words, a SeedQR, a private key, an encrypted backup or a sealed vault, by camera, paste or share.</li>
<li>Signs, or holds any key that can spend.</li>
<li>Opens a network connection or a link, or writes to the clipboard.</li>
<li>Backs anything up to a cloud or copies it to a new phone.</li>
</ul>

<b>Permissions</b>
It asks for the camera and for the phone's unlock prompt, and for nothing else. Wallet descriptions are kept encrypted and open only after you unlock the phone. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it kept.

<b>Requirements</b>
Android 8.0 or later, on an arm64 phone.
